# Research direction

**Scalable Skill Acquisition × Architecture-Aware Reinforcement Learning**

## Goal

Investigate original learning algorithms that improve acquisition, composition,
and transfer of previously difficult reasoning skills. The exact method is undecided.

## Questions to resolve

- How should a reasoning skill and its difficulty be operationally defined?
- Which architectural properties could inform learning or credit assignment?
- How should acquisition, composition, and transfer be measured separately?
- Which baselines and matched compute budgets permit meaningful comparisons?

## Evidence standard

Select tasks, datasets, models, baselines, metrics, splits, and compute budgets
before evaluating a method. Isolate held-out tests from training and tuning.
Report uncertainty across seeds, failure cases, and actual compute use.

## Current status

Problem framing only. No method, architecture, benchmark, dependency stack, or
compute allocation has been selected. No literature claims or experimental results
have been established in this repository.
