Continue existing candidates and lineage. Do not restart, abandon or prematurely
narrow them. Separate demonstrated capability from the computational or learning
mechanism that produces it. Expandable memory, calls, composition and learned
representations alone are already available capabilities, not foundational
contributions.

For every serious candidate address:
1. Actual new computational/learning mechanism.
2. Important limitation it could overcome.
3. Why it matters beyond the current example.
4. Comparable existing neural/program-synthesis/learning methods and resources.
5. Broader problem class made tractable, efficient or easier to learn if it works.

No requirement of a new primitive, non-neural implementation or impossibility
proof against existing architectures. Known operations can have a consequential
new organization. Invention must not be replaced by endless prior-art checking
or benchmark tuning. Continue autonomous mathematical construction, experiments
and substantive revision; do not fabricate breakthroughs.

ingredients, not a proof that an entire new coupling is already implemented.
