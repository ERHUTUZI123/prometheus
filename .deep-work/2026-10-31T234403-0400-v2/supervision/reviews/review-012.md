## Strong rival and the finite scientific frontier

**RECONSTRUCTION — an exact limitation of this task interface:** once the measured generator tables are available, every exact word action is their product. The task reward reads only that endpoint permutation. Thus the entire word→task-output map factors through the measured finite library. The library already generates S4. The certified epsilon update leaves that entire exact map unchanged; continuous trace geometry supplies no additional information about a word's discrete answer in this contract. Here the sampled J decrease is tiny, observed A/C step counts do not change, and no later-acquisition comparison or learned geometric organization is implemented.
