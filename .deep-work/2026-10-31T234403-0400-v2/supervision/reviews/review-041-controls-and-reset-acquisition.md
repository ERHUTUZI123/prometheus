## Reset V1: the port works, the required new function is not learned

The extension freezes the actually acquired old core, adding48 loop-head and8 call-head parameters. The86 total include30 frozen old parameters;34 buffer scalars and source geometry are additional state. A24-generator program is realized over all nine roots of z⁹−9z=u. A native pair port encodes(core,fresh blank) as3·core+0 and decodes by quotient/remainder. Each selected pair call allocates a new ternary cell. Subsequent gates do not read old garbage, so projecting each pair permutation to its fresh-cell logical channel gives the correct evolving core marginal. Inverse cleanup checks the full lift hash and namespace, not just its logical channel. The frozen version includes the matching Soft/Native history-capacity check identified before execution.

The six TRAIN examples contain three singleton new-operation observations and their old-operation suffixes. They identify a logical reset-to-zero but supply no joint permutation, garbage code, correct call, or target loop. The old operations/readout remain frozen throughout. Thus old-task success here is retention by that disclosed frozen frame, not evidence of a new shared-plasticity advantage.

Actual outcomes are:

- Initial TRAIN neural/native1/3; final TRAIN neural0/native1/3.
- Final held-out neural1/6/native1/3; old neural/native1.
- No admission and no own-call maintenance trigger.
- All100 updates execute:91 full-scale, four half-scale, five quarter-scale;14 proposals are rejected.
- The mean logical channel remains a bijection at every recorded point: identity for9, [2,1,0] for91. It **never forms a many-to-one mean channel**. Final lift is[6,0,2,3,4,5,1,7,8].

This is a class-formation failure, not merely final wrong routing. At the final mean, every available logical bank operation is bijective, so selecting any one cannot reset all three starts. The unchanged near-permutation encoder/readout and mixtures of these permutation channels also cannot supply a high-confidence reset for all starts. Calling repair alone cannot supply the missing many-to-one operation.

All24 event coordinates were sampled.55 selected coefficients are zero; the others span−19.2965 to19.8241. These values are from changing parameters and different coordinates: their spread is **not** a controlled gradient-variance estimate or proof that variance caused failure. The event estimator itself has the correct conditional form, multiplying its one uniformly selected coordinate by1/q=24. Averaging four independent full estimates is a justified, prospective diagnostic; it incurs approximately four times gradient/terminal work per transaction and is not a bug fix or an equal-compute result. Its outcome remains pending this review.

The grammar is not shown incapable of the function. The successful ordinary lift's actual six-generator word[0,2,0,1,5,1] embeds in the same24-slot order at positions0,2,8,9,13,17; observed inactive chamber maps are identities. Inactive/active amplitudes7/9 and the nonzero frozen feature permit an in-box head representation. This is a post hoc representability construction from actual conventional discovery, **not an executed neural result and not a program to donate to the learner**. It localizes an unachieved learning/mean-policy step without changing the task or pretending that representability ensures learnability.
