## 4. Constructive next cost repair: proof-qualified class reuse across omega

Human priority is whether continued topological capability growth lowers parameter, training or protection costs. A concrete authorized repair targets the current self-imposed execution expense: the current lift cache is invalidated whenever omega changes, even though the declared shear family already proves the ideal endpoint action unchanged. Eliminating that repeated work is a legitimate method improvement, initially measured as removing our own overhead rather than presumed superiority to direct computation.

For a correct implementation, the cache API should satisfy:

1. **Semantic key.** Include D, fixed base-fiber/sheet identification, the learned primitive class or canonical braid word, compiler/cover/representation version and the certified deformation-family namespace. Omega need not be part of that semantic key. In the CURRENT canonical compiler, permutation determines its selected word; for a future full-braid representation, equal permutations alone do not imply equal braids.
2. **Qualified reuse.** On a hit, verify finite admissible omega and unchanged endpoint-fixed global diffeomorphism family. The map must be a valid D-permutation under the same sheet labels. New learned class, D growth, changed cover/basepoint, compiler, semantic sheet identification or relevant family assumptions require an appropriate new entry/transport or invalidation. Ordinary decoder/call/encoder weight changes do not by themselves invalidate the raw map when sheet labels stay fixed; their task-binding effects still need the separate capability contract. Reusing an old map does not protect a changed neural call/input binding.
3. **Execution honesty.** Count actual_first_lift separately from certified_family_reuse. A hit does not mean a new numerical trajectory was executed. It substitutes the certified ideal operation semantics. If a finite numerical or physical executor is still claimed at the new omega, its separate error/separation/speed contract must remain satisfied; ideal homotopy alone does not certify arbitrary finite tracking errors.
4. **Numerical margin.** Current real-arithmetic bounds give delta>=4/(1+B), V<=(1+B)3pi+pi H/T. With coordinate error epsilon, a sufficient conservative continuation condition is V*dt+2epsilon<delta/4. The portable floating-point epsilon itself needs its stated evidence/assumption; sampled success is not an outward-rounded certificate. No error-free physical control may be inferred from a digital cache hit.
5. **Persistence and full costs.** A persisted cache must carry its namespace, class map, base/family certificate data and origin actual-lift evidence/version. Reload must validate them rather than trust an opaque protected flag. If entries are not persisted, rebuilding after reload is paid. Bound entries and count storage, hashing, proof checks, first realizations, misses, evictions and invalidations. Keep prior acquisition/maintenance work in its historical ledger.
6. **Fair direct control.** Grant the direct executor equivalent map/class caching and maintenance opportunities. A valid family cache can reduce digital repeated integration/protection work; it does not eliminate physical path action/energy or automatically yield an acquisition advantage. Actual growth must charge new-class and new-D work.

These are constructive mathematical/software conditions, not a new approval gate. The homotopy proof genuinely justifies ideal class reuse; the resource test should quantify its savings against the fair direct implementation and distinguish cold acquisition from steady-state reuse. This cost repair leaves the remaining acquisition link explicit: current new class content is obtained through terminal-history/permutation formation, then realized geometrically. The next major claim requires a real rule or measured resource consequence that changes that linkage, not relabeling the successful conventional formation package.
