## Independent mathematical reconstruction

    mu1=1/2-(1-rho)D,        mu2=1/2+rho*D,
    D^2=a^2/4+(1-a^2)/(12*rho*(1-rho)).

Their four ordered endpoints are mu1 +/- w1/2 and mu2 +/- w2/2. Writing r=rho*(1-rho), integration gives

    M1 = sum integral_interval (1-2x) dx = 0,
    M2 = a[1/2-6rD^2-a^2(rho^3+(1-rho)^3)/2] = 0,

because rho^3+(1-rho)^3=1-3r. Thus total interval length is a and both distortion moments vanish exactly. This is a nontrivial motion of the actual turning points, not merely a relabeling of the same curve.
