## V2 has a correct contract and a missing formation coupling

The incumbent/reference interface is legitimate and substantial. It supplies trusted internal tuples from an already acquired computation, equally to rivals and with its cost charged. It does not supply external truth the incumbent never learned. Runtime U/R lacks the clean reference. Repairing physical deviations preserves systematic incumbent errors and does not bypass the earlier endpoint-query bound.

The local basin contract is sufficient for finite DAG or recurrent-step induction. It permits correlated inputs and repeated fan-out; no independence of their logical errors is needed. It does require valid initial blocks, the stated physical fault locations and an actual admitted contract for every invoked operation. Finite training agreement does not establish those universal premises or unseen-state coverage.

For sevenfold Boolean coding, one input error plus one U output fault gives at most two errors for NOT. Two input blocks plus one U fault give at most three for coordinatewise AND, and that bound is attainable. Every nonfaulty majority-of-seven output returns the correct bit; one R output fault renews radius one. The restorer is physically represented. Each AND costs 14 neurons and 63 input wires; NOT has 56 wires. Encoder formation, reference, failed search, routing and version migration are additional. The majority neuron needs fan-in seven or a separately counted noisy implementation.

The pair union bound is also correct: at most `21p²` for violation of each seven-neuron stage's one-fault budget, and at most `min(1,42Hp²)` across H invocations. It is a conservative sufficient-event bound, not indefinite reliability. Correlated physical failures, noisy initial encoding and the final scalar readout require their own treatment; a last independently flipping scalar retains its p floor.

The score-function proposal is valid for the stated finite stochastic objective when actual pre-fault decisions/inputs are recorded, the exogenous fault law is parameter-independent and the baseline is decision-independent conditional on its information. Arbitrary completed-trajectory baselines can bias it. Continuous stochastic-logit optimization does not make deterministic parameter quantization differentiable; the frozen gates need their own checks. When final output faults can target any R output, clean restoration must lie within distance `t−e_R` of its target. Thus t=e_R=1 requires exact clean restoration, as the witness provides.

The independent audit's main attack is decisive for the advertised v2 law. Under a fixed q and fixed bank, once all relevant distances exceed 2q, the entire distance deficit is zero. Raising failure weights cannot trigger another bit. Operation error amplification can then worsen without causing representation growth through that objective. The seven-bit result is therefore fixed-q code construction plus circuit learning; it is not yet demonstrated operation-selected guard demand.

The proposed amplification/Y-set repairs address this concretely. An actual possible pre-restoration set can create an incompatible restoration demand, and an operation-specific certified radius can change the separation requirement. A sampled maximum is only a lower witness of needed guard, not an upper certificate. Moreover output repair cannot recover two required meanings from the same corrupted incoming observation: their conditional output distributions are identical. Code growth must repair that upstream collision or change the information/fault contract. A bad chosen U is not evidence that every implementation needs more code bits; paid U/R refitting must compete with growth. These constraints make the repair meaningful, rather than forbid it.

The projection-five/AND-seven calculation is valid for the declared coordinatewise realization. It is neither a universal redundancy lower bound nor free conversion between differently sized interfaces. A conventional compiler derives the same scoped savings and seven-bit witness from the same inspected graph or legal reference queries. That witness is not a discriminating acquisition experiment.

## V3 supplies a different, valid representation law

The constructor appends actual missing Boolean pullbacks `phi composed with F`. For a finite family of distinct functions and bijective F, the pullback is injective on functions. Exact closure makes it a permutation of the finite coordinate family. The runtime operation therefore transports every Hamming error pattern without amplifying it. This stronger reliability property does not follow merely from a clean linear lift.

The eight-coordinate counterexample checks. A single wrong xy-helper bit is retained and also flips all four z-containing coordinates. Its five-error result is one bit from the wrong-z codeword. A nearest-code restorer can therefore confidently restore the wrong value even though the clean update is correct and the storage code separates states.

The eleven-coordinate repair also checks. Toffoli exchanges the four z-linear forms with `q_ab=z⊕a x⊕b y⊕xy` and fixes the remaining three. CNOT gives `q_ab composed with C=q_(a⊕b⊕1,b)` and permutes the original linear forms. The two semantic updates do not commute. For two states differing only in z the code distance is eight; differing (x,y) gives four old plus two new differences, hence minimum distance six. The same code supports arbitrary words in this particular noncommuting library.

The noisy correction proof is physical. Each of eleven outputs has four private radius-two codeword detectors and one OR, giving 55 threshold neurons. Detector fan-in is eleven; a substrate with a smaller cap needs a new counted implementation. The clean codeword detectors are mutually exclusive within the admitted radius. One R-neuron fault can affect only its private output. Eleven noisy permutation/copy outputs transport one incoming error plus at most one new fault to at most two errors; R then renews radius one.

The cost is 66 neurons per corrected invocation before selectors/encoding, with 528 R input wires plus eleven U input wires. The corresponding sufficient-budget pair union bound is `min(1,1540Hp²)`, since `C(11,2)+C(55,2)=1540`. This valid local proof is not a measured acquisition result or an automatic stochastic improvement over a smaller circuit. Enlarging the physical circuit increases fault opportunities.

Thus v3 genuinely repairs v2's operation-to-representation gap. Its growth selects specific nonlinear functions, not only a larger generic code. The worst-case cost theorem below does not invalidate this favorable small family, and difficulty of learning the circuits is not itself a refutation.

## The scaling attack concerns the actual representation, with a physical rival

For N=2^m states, the increment cycle c and swap of 0/1 have O(m)-size ordinary bounded-fan-in Boolean circuits. Ripple carry gives increment; a test that all higher bits are zero controls the low-bit swap. No ancilla-free reversible-gate claim is needed. Conjugating the swap by cycle powers supplies adjacent transpositions and generates S_N. Finite forward closure includes inverse/group words.

A Boolean feature with k nonzero values has exactly `C(N,k)` distinct pullbacks under S_N. Any injective code contains a nonconstant feature, so exact closed coordinate-permutation representation requires at least N coordinates. Retaining an initial balanced bit requires `C(N,N/2)`, approximately `2^N/sqrt(N)`. Duplicate coordinates do not evade this; abandoning the balanced feature can avoid the stronger bound but not the N-coordinate minimum. This is a hand proof of exact representation size, not a learning lower bound or a statement about all codes, approximate/local closure or arbitrary neural architectures.

The alternative also has actual unreliable gates. Root's 13-bit bitwise compiler admits two input errors per logical block. Coordinatewise AND gives at most four input-derived errors and two U faults give at most six, within majority-of-thirteen's correct region. Thirteen private output circuits implement that majority using a finite fan-in-two Boolean circuit each. At most two physical faults inside R affect at most two outputs, renewing the allowance. The constants may be large and are not hidden; they are independent of m. Compiling the O(m) incumbent circuit gives O(m) gate/state costs times those constants and handles the candidate's two faults per whole invocation as a special case. Reliable wires/addressing/scheduling are shared assumptions. No same-p practical comparison with the three-bit witness is asserted.

This is stronger than a universal-simulation objection: a specific short-operation family makes the proposed exact global closure exponentially larger than an explicit comparable-fault compiler. It rules out the unqualified broad compactness prospect of this rule. It leaves restricted small orbits and different representations open.

## Prior boundaries and allocation judgment

[Judd–Munro](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/1992/file/c361bc7b2c033a83d663b8d9fb4be56e-Paper.pdf) learns noise-shaped encoder/decoder codes, including structured faults with a reliable extra decoder. Neither inspected source is attributed this exact acquired U/R loop.

[FCC Definitions1–5/Theorem1](https://arxiv.org/html/2102.03094) already formalizes function-dependent separation and irregular redundancy distances, with a known function/encoding and channel errors. It does not itself provide physically faulty repeated execution. [Brunton et al.](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0150171) constructs or identifies additional nonlinear observables and iterates toward invariant closure. Arbitrary linear closure is not v3's permutation condition; its continuous-state restrictions must not be transplanted here. Historical coverage of every v3 detail remains OPEN.

Calling the same pullback algorithm an ordinary orbit compiler is also partly an algorithm re-description, not proof that a published method already contains every proposed coupling. This review does not close by that tautology or require unfamiliar primitives. The actual allocation reasons are the central exact-global cost obstruction and the remaining restricted success counterfactual: known incumbent programs and seed observables generate a finite compiled representation; neural fitting is optional, and no important capability-acquisition or resource consequence beyond that compilation has yet been supplied after the serious repair. The valid small example cannot establish that missing effect.

An intermediate rule might trade exact isometry for bounded error spread, discover smaller interacting blocks or use approximate closure. Naming those possibilities is not a constructed successor. Ordinary fault-tolerant compilation and matched noise-aware code/operation learning remain strong alternatives; preserving existing value distinctions and paying re-encoding also remain necessary. I presently have no further concrete rule from these passes that warrants another implementation or repair cycle. This is not a demand for a cheap verifier, a universal separation theorem or a breakthrough at every checkpoint.

Retain the operation-compatible reliability question as OPEN, preserve the finite positive and cost results, and checkpoint the current allocation.
