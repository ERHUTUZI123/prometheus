## Authorized repair and interpretation

ROOT selected one execution repair: internal FP64 row normalization, Gram/RHS/ridge assembly, `solve_ex`, signed value mix, then cast read to FP32; parameters, episode-state/caller dtype, lambda and numerical tolerances remain fixed. It requires a **new same-execution-policy CPU reference**, explicit outside-capture checks of all 16 forward solve-info tensors, separate source/receipt and complete cost accounting. Forward info success alone does not prove backward or whole-NN stability. The old FP32 numerical/capture failure remains failed evidence regardless of the repair's outcome. No `solve_ex` or FP64 result has been assumed by this review.

The v1a discrepancy is compatible with sensitivity of the conditioned recurrent read, but the current evidence does not isolate every numerical source. The selected repair is a bounded implementation test, not tuning lambda, selecting a favorable task score or authorizing a trained arm. GPU training, learned query binding and broad capability–complete-cost superiority remain unestablished. Primary remains unfrozen and the completed three-arm training count remains 7,200.
