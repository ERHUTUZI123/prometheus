## Fresh v1: a real semantic repair, independently checked

The repair changes the acquired object to a bilinear composition table and its operand dimension. This is genuinely different from recompiling an unchanged function. It should be retained as a useful distinction.

I reconstructed the complete worked case directly:

```
A = [[1,0],[0,-1]],  B = [[0,1],[1,0]],
C = AB = [[0,1],[-1,0]].
```

Then `A^2=B^2=I`, `AB=C`, `BA=-C`, `C^2=-I`, `AC=B`, `CA=-B`, `BC=-A`, `CB=A`. These agree with every displayed multiplication coefficient. The four response columns for `I,A,B,AB` are mutually orthogonal and nonzero in the stated order. Thus the fourth behavior cannot be represented in the original three-dimensional **linear decoder span**. The associativity coefficient equations are correctly indexed, and operator composition supplies a direct associative realization. `(AB)^n` has the stated four-cycle.

This is hand-checked finite algebra, not an executed probe or proof-assistant certificate. Its dimension witness relies on the supplied linear operation class and complete spanning probes. It is not a lower bound on all nonlinear neural encodings, all sampled behavior or arbitrary future state types. The candidate scopes this correctly.

The strong rival is fully explicit and uses the same observations. Queries at the two basis vectors identify every entry of A and B. Store ordinary two-by-two matrices and multiply them. This gives AB, all later products and every held-out word without learning a new structure tensor. The candidate's four operation coordinates have the same scalar count as a two-by-two matrix; its displayed bilinear product has no demonstrated arithmetic scaling advantage.
