## Failed-trace splices: a real acquisition event, insufficient selection evidence

The causal proposal has a grounded information interface. An actual patched continuation is scored against the original raw task and emitted answer. It receives no latent correctness oracle or supplied high-level counterfactual label. Successful splices can train a new donor-free residual transition; forced-call tests, sham patches, construction-cost repayment and read/write interface growth are substantive conditions. This is more than causal interpretation of a fixed correct model.

The displayed full-adder splice is algebraically correct under its favorable coordinate/error assumptions, but the same-info feature adapter can select the two correct components directly. Its success would not distinguish the proposed acquisition route. Root's independent-component calculation is also correct: a fixed splice has unconditional success probability p^k, and conditioning on both donors failing gives the displayed `p^k(1-p^b)(1-p^(k-b))/(1-p^k)^2`. The equality-only q/2^k counting argument is valid only in its explicitly uninformative uniform-target assay. Neither is a lower bound for structured tasks or adaptive, informative contexts.

Prediction conflicts require the qualification already recorded by root: conditional target variance obstructs exact squared-state imitation, not necessarily a valid deterministic operation. Two different patches can both be successful, and their mean can fail. A task-grounded selector may suffice without reconstructing every donor detail.

There is a concrete source comparison, without declaring complete coverage: [Model-Based Trajectory Stitching §3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.04280) learns connecting actions between previously unconnected observed states and behaviorally clones improved trajectories. The present latent splice can create a previously unobserved internal state and requires executable installation, so these are not identical mechanisms. Nevertheless, successful recombinations followed by imitation already have a strong acquisition precedent. The present context-witness rule has not identified an economical structural premise that changes that comparison. PARK is therefore an allocation judgment, not proof that this route is impossible or fully anticipated.

## Generated interaction laws: retain the architectural hypothesis

The higher-order candidate changes actual execution organization. A pair of generated agent codes emits new agents, codes and interface-preserving port wiring. Binding wires can become subsequent execution sites; the runtime does not call a supplied lambda evaluator or host substitution routine. That is a meaningful representation/transition choice even though graph reduction and its confluence discipline are established.

**VERIFIED as a direct derivation:** for frozen pair-local emissions, distinct active pairs have disjoint agents; their codes and decisions are unchanged by the other's rewrite. Gluing the two replacement graphs commutes, including cyclic wires. The local diamond and finite-normalizing-witness/random-descent argument therefore support the stated uncensored result. The worked three-event affine higher-order trace is coherent. The three boundary matchings really are indistinguishable on the stated identity-only observer; the opaque-constructor example distinguishes them. Neither hand derivation is a learning result, and the tiny matching test is not admitted.

The source boundary is specific. [Lafont §§1–2](https://chorasimilarity.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/ic-lafont-1.pdf) supplies the local interaction discipline and explains why naive duplication does not copy every executable package; its code/copier/decoder construction matters. [Neural Production Systems §3.1](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/file/d785bf9067f8af9e078b93cf26de2b54-Paper.pdf) learns pair-conditioned slot updates and rule selection, but the inspected mechanism does not perform principal-pair port surgery. [RewriteNets §3](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-ijcnlp.26.pdf) proposes learned fixed-bank string patterns/replacements and a conflict-selection layer; that is not the generated-code interaction protocol. No empirical or confluence claim from that paper is adopted here.

Calling a hypothetical learner with exactly the new K, encoder and optimizer a conventional rival does not establish that an existing architecture already contains the proposal. Compiling its neural arithmetic to a program also does not prove equal acquisition cost. Conversely, continuous/generated codes do not establish new functionality, and known confluence is not a new learning estimator. The actual intersection remains **OPEN**: can pure local generated semantics make reusable execution organization learnable while compatibility under independent execution is enforced structurally?

There is a plausible reason to investigate that intersection: learning need not discover or maintain a global schedule to define the computation, and a learned local protocol could govern new graph sizes and generated representations. This is not a factorial search advantage, a variance theorem, or a promise that sparse terminal reward can discover an interpreter. A useful architecture may use ordinary gradient/score-function training; requiring a novel estimator in advance would wrongly exclude architecture-level discoveries.

## Stochastic and fuel repairs: correct scope, limited scientific role

The ancestry-keyed field provides pathwise correspondence under independent reordering. An ideal sequential iid stream already preserves the distribution under the stated nonanticipating conditions; keyed randomness is not itself new task information. For bounded complete causal configurations, the product likelihood and event-score identity are the ordinary likelihood derivation. They do not prove easier acquisition than an actual fixed-schedule learner.

The local-fuel repair gives a real finite-state discipline. Each accepted rewrite reduces total fuel, so accepted events and live agents are bounded. Independent acceptance and rewiring decisions commute because they read only their own pair. Disabled principal pairs cannot be recharged through another pair under the stated rules. Termination then supplies a common fuel-augmented normal form. Worklist bookkeeping and closed-wire/ancestry storage still need their separate costs. No primary claim of novel affine/resource semantics is supported.

Two implementation obligations remain explicit for a future version: sampled proposals that disable a pair must contribute their likelihood scores if the loss depends on those outcomes; and peak live space can vary across schedules even when the terminal graph and additive event work agree. An `N_peak` loss does not inherit the narrower terminal-plus-additive schedule-invariance statement. Fuel may guarantee a common finite bound while stranding useful work. This repair solves operational consistency, not the formation problem, and is not a reason to promote resource allocation as the contribution.
