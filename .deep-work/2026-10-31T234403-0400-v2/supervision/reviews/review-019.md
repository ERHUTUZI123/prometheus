## The teaching construction establishes a real outcome distinction

Synthetic targets are proposed control signals, not secretly correct intermediate labels. The teacher may inspect developing state, and the comparator must receive that same information. All lessons, gradients, histories, inspection and replay are costs.

The scalar calculation is correct. `B(a,b,d)=(b,d,d)` and `B²(a,b,d)=(d,d,d)`. Present, first-child and second-child execution losses therefore inspect a, b and d respectively. At target1 and (1,1,0), the first two measured losses and gradients vanish while the second-child loss supplies the missing d gradient. Current task performance, construction of one task-capable child and transmission of teaching competence are distinct operational outcomes.

## The recipe repair changes future learning, and has an exact concrete rival

V2 genuinely changes representation. It retains a finite recipe ξ, constructs `w=D(ξ)` through actual SGD, then changes ξ using later task loss. With a frozen constructor and initialization/seed during this update and Euclidean recipe descent,

`delta w=−rho J_D J_Dᵀ grad_w L + O(rho²)`.

The metric and nullspace may differ for identical current w. This is a real causal distinction between execution equivalence and learning-interface equivalence. It does not automatically favor the recipe: a singular Jacobian can block a directly available update, scaling can imitate a different learning rate, and regeneration/storage have costs.

The worked calculation checks exactly. One unit-rate lesson for a zero-initialized scalar student gives `D(x,y)=xy`. Writing e=xy−t,

`x'=x−rho e y; y'=y−rho e x; w'=xy−rho e(x²+y²)+rho²e²xy`.

At w=1, t=0 and rho=1/10, recipes (1,1) and (2,1/2) give 81/100 and 117/200. Their first-order metrics are 2 and 17/4. Ordinary gradient descent on the factorized model `w=xy` gives those same finite updates, with the same two stored scalars, and can compile regeneration into multiplication. This is actual finite-step coverage of the supplied repair, not universal simulation or a fictional historical clone of a new architecture.

[Dataset Distillation §§3.1–3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/1811.10959) already makes weights a differentiable result of synthetic training data and optimizes that data through the real update. [LEO §§2.3.1–2.3.5](https://arxiv.org/html/1807.05960) decodes latent coordinates to weights, adapts those coordinates through the decoder and meta-trains the generator. These are concrete precedents for the recipe/geometry mechanism. Their inspected methods do not establish coverage of every possible self-transmitted teacher. V2 explicitly abandons that coupling as its distinguishing property, and its actual replacement supplies no remaining important learning effect beyond this latent/recipe organization. That is why closure follows after the repair, rather than merely from the phrase meta-learning.

## The query audit repairs the information premise

For invertible A over F₂ and sole endpoint AND(Ax), the unique positive is `s=A⁻¹1`, uniformly distributed on the `N=2^m−1` nonzero points under uniform A. Before a hit, adaptive queries follow one all-negative branch. With a hard pathwise q-query cap, hit probability is at most min(q,N)/N; one final unverified guess gives at most min(q+1,N)/N identification probability. Conditioning on random seeds proves the randomized bound, with the stated minimax qualifications. An expected query budget cannot be substituted for the hard cap.

Known **aligned** rank-r rows B impose `Bs=1`; for r≥1, the completion count makes s uniform on a coset of size `2^(m−r)`. The r=0 exception, arbitrary-mask syndrome and extra-side-information qualifications are essential and correct. Even a full executable old coordinate basis does not identify the syndrome of a new independent AND. Observing the complete current AND function also generally fails to identify its ordered hidden factors.

Thus compact execution state does not imply informative acquisition evidence. Own-model gradients and synthetic lessons computed from an all-negative transcript do not distinguish its remaining worlds. Genuine retained information, component/Hamming feedback, paid counterexamples or positives can change that premise. The audit establishes this finite boundary; it is not a new foundational mechanism, a prohibition on self-inspection or a reason to run another parity example.
