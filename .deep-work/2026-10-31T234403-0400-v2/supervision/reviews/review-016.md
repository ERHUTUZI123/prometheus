## The witness survives the immediate mechanism audit

The input is genuinely only a token chain and Start. Parsing, continuations, closure creation, lookup and copying occur through the listed bounded pair rewrites. The fixed tokenizer and prefix source language remain supplied information. They do not supply a host parser, binder aliases or environment stack.

The consistent polarity assignment is positive data/continuation constructors and negative controllers, including PReturn and Ret. Under that assignment every listed active pair is legal. Each external boundary occurs once, new wires occur twice, and every replacement stays within four agents and two auxiliaries. In particular, Eval/App branches its owned environment through D; Apply/Closure obtains its parent from the captured environment; matching/nonmatching Lookup preserve or erase the appropriate owned branches. I found no invalid successful-path row.

The printed stream has 25 tokens and 15 syntax nodes. The `n+m+2` parser count therefore gives 42 events, as stated. For a passive constructor tree, summing `2+arity` gives `3N-1` cumulative copy allocations; summing arities gives `N-1` eraser allocations. These are hand derivations, not measured neural/runtime costs. `3N-1` is not the final live size of the two copied trees.

Ownership gives a valid restricted answer to the duplication obstacle: the protocol copies acyclic data descriptions of syntax, captured environments and values, then activates them through Eval. It does not copy arbitrary running or cyclic nets. With streaming copies, a child endpoint can still have a pending D producer; the ownership invariant should be read as an eventual owned data description, or evaluated under the explicitly allowed finish-copy-first explanatory schedule. It does not authorize copying a controller. The independent support audit reaches the same concrete boundary; its agreement is not an additional execution result.

The scope argument is correct for these manually supplied rows. Applying the two F copies creates frames beneath captured x=a rather than beneath caller x=b. Nearest-frame lookup implements shadowing. This is a familiar closure/environment implementation, now made explicit inside the local law.

[Sinot §§3.4–4](https://lsv.ens-paris-saclay.fr/Publis/PAPERS/PDF/sinot-tlca05.pdf) already constructs token-passing evaluation with copying/erasure; its translation supplies syntax and binder backpointers. That covers the broad evaluator organization, while it does not establish learning the present raw-stream protocol from terminal feedback. The distinction belongs to acquisition, rather than a claim that this reference evaluator is new.

## Three distinctions the next construction must preserve

**Names versus lexical scope.** Root's finite correspondence argument is valid for the equality/copy/fresh interface, with fresh allocations compared up to an extended renaming. It does not provide a deterministic equivariant fresh-name selector. [Gabbay/Pitts §§2–3](https://www.gabbay.org.uk/papers/newaas.pdf) makes the permutation, support and freshness distinction precise. Root's earliest-binding counterexample also checks: global renaming preserves that wrong policy, while scoped alpha-renaming exposes it. Thus covariance is an acquisition bias over identifiers, not already learned binding.

**Two physical calls versus evidence that reuse is necessary.** The handwritten F is constant. Its trace really contains two independently constructed calls, but one call produces the same terminal answer. The example distinguishes capture from dynamic scope; it does not establish acquisition of generally reusable higher-order behavior. No single output uniquely identifies an internal evaluator, since valid simplification/sharing may compute it differently. Later mechanism claims need interventions on actually formed links with capability-matched controls.

**Output availability versus completed execution.** An Atom may reach the output before independent D/Erase work finishes. Before implementation, either drain to the declared normal form with an explicit disabled/error policy, or state a lazy observer and charge retained garbage plus cleanup/reset separately. Early readability cannot inherit the complete-normal-form work/schedule statement. This is an unfinished observation interface, not a discovered incorrect rewrite.

## The environment-growth attack is consequential

Root's family follows the actual rows. Each new let closure owns one copy of the previous environment, while its parent frame owns another, plus three constructors. Starting at N0=1 gives `N_i=2N_(i-1)+3=4*2^i-3`. The copying events alone sum to `4(2^k-1)-3k`. I also read root's later full-cleanup qualification: the finite finish-copy-first normalization plus the strong diamond gives the same complete event count under every maximal normalization of this frozen deterministic witness. Streaming can change peak space, but cannot remove this exponential total interaction work. Early output, censoring and new copy/erase fusion would be different semantics. The family is linear in source size and contains neither recursion nor cyclic values.

Immutable heap environments can share the previous environment with O(k) objects on this family. That is a concrete execution-organization comparison; it is not an equal-acquisition-cost proof or evidence that NRAM already learns this evaluator. The current owned-tree witness cannot support an efficiency claim merely because every collision has bounded footprint.

A learner could instead acquire selective capture or an explicit sharing/reference protocol. Those are known implementation ideas and would need their actual local state/transitions and costs, especially because a linear port graph does not get arbitrary pointer fanout for free. Hand-implementing them would remain a witness. Increasing fuel or ignoring cleanup does not constitute a repair. A first limited acquisition result need not already achieve optimal sharing, but its copying cost and restricted claim must be stated rather than hidden.

## Actual conventional alternatives narrow, but do not close, the prospect

[NRAM §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.06392) learns register/circuit control from I/O using supplied arithmetic, equality and memory modules; its READ/distribution work and conditional discretization are material costs. Neural GPU already learns a shared local transition from raw sequence/output examples without a symbolic evaluator. [NLI §§2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.18907v1) jointly learns latent program tokens and their recurrent neural execution from I/O specifications. None of those inspected mechanisms establishes this exact pair-local mutable-link architecture or an equal learning cost for lexical evaluation. They do establish that raw-I/O learning, learned primitive semantics, pointer control or lack of teacher traces alone are insufficient distinctions.

The remaining high-risk hypothesis is specific: exact locally generated communication links may make reusable scope/value/control organization accessible under honest formation and execution budgets. A hypothetical identical-K learner is not historical coverage. Equally, merely implementing the witness, naming code values differently or selecting a few reference rows would not test that hypothesis. Ordinary gradient/score-function training remains legitimate; a novel optimizer is not an admission prerequisite.

The nominal and feedback interfaces need an actual choice. Original Neural GPU has finite embeddings; it does not automatically transport unseen opaque references. Original NRAM admits integer information beyond equality. Use matched finite-vocabulary inputs, explicitly equal integer access, or clearly named/costed nominal variants. If target atom identity is supplied as a label, methods may use their different gradient routes from that same label. If only an exact-match reward is returned, giving a comparator the hidden target identity would add information. These choices must precede a comparison, not be repaired after a result.

## ONE next constructive learning target

**Develop acquisition-v2 around acquiring a captured computational value that can be reused under shadowing, with a declared total-work envelope.** A concrete extensional family is: bind n to an ordinary raw-lambda definition of Church negation; create `f=(lambda b. n b)`; shadow n with Identity; then observe `(f^j True) A B` for j=1 and j=2. Correct captured n gives B then A, while dynamic caller binding gives A then A. Vary Boolean inputs, raw names, irrelevant scopes and surrounding compositions so a fixed atom or repetition shortcut cannot fit the target family. Not/True/Identity are source terms, not host primitives or supplied local rules.

This is one coupled burden: forming and retaining the behavior of a captured function while creating useful execution/copy organization. It can be paired with the environment-growth condition when testing a resource claim, rather than using a constant-output family as an acquisition benchmark. It is not a new benchmark contribution, an assertion that these examples identify the complete evaluator, or an experiment authorization.

The acquisition construction must show how actual allowed terminal evidence changes the shared emitter, how unsuccessful/invalid emitted graphs are handled, what the curriculum supplies, and what prevents success from meaning only identification of this handwritten table. No teacher rule IDs, correct intermediate graphs or uncharged evaluator may enter. A finite central test and the actual Neural GPU/NRAM comparison must be frozen before major implementation. A useful partial result may state its scaffold and limits; it should not be rejected merely because every helper primitive was not learned autonomously.

The prospect remains consequential enough for this serious formation pass. If the strongest actual acquisition proposal only selects a few supplied rule choices or installs a familiar parser/closure implementation, reframe before training. If it gives a credible local learning bias for acquiring and transferring the generated organization, a later bounded test can assess it.
