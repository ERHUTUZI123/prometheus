## This is an actual acquisition proposal

The parameterization is more than a name for a future learner. A finite-bit shared neural emitter produces node descriptors, codes, reference selectors and a legal boundary matching. No named evaluator roles or reference rule table are supplied to its search. Holding W fixed makes a candidate an executable, coherent interaction law throughout all its recursive uses. New emitted codes are possible, although their semantics still comes from that same W.

The once-per-law estimator is correct for the stated finite distribution and bounded fitness. The baseline must be independent of the sampled law conditional on the bank/history; with that condition, the score is `grad log q(l,W)` once per draw. There is no per-event stochastic-emission score to sum. The conditional repeated-choice probability calculation explains a failure of fresh event noise. It supplies no neural success-mass bound and no advantage over competitors that also hold sampled parameters fixed.

The trace-edit route is substantive too. It hypothesizes an emission at an actually visited local input, compiles that hypothesis into neural weights with a metered CE/KL/proximity objective, quantizes, then tests the complete new law from fresh raw initial states. Full replay correctly exposes interference at other inputs and repeated uses. Its internal CE target is a proposal, not a correct semantic label. Provisional successful-trace anchors can be wrong. The stated separate proposal score for a learned edit selector is necessary; a distilled W' cannot be treated as a direct sample from the whole-weight distribution without its actual sampling probability.

Consequently, calling this only “there is no learning rule” would be false. It is an unexecuted neural graph-program induction algorithm. It can in principle discover a representation unlike the hand witness. Ordinary optimizers are legitimate, and no new gradient theorem is required. What remains unestablished is why this organization makes a consequential mechanism easier to acquire or transfer at its full cost.

The complete-drain observer, correctness-dominant bounded fitness and declared retention boundary are also improvements. Saving W_old is rollback of an entire law, not preservation by W_new or cross-law closure composition. V2 states that distinction correctly. No stronger continual-retention conclusion should be imported into the present offline acquisition question.

## The cost repair is real and has a sharp boundary

Exact capture must be applied to **every** lambda, including the let-function wrappers. On the original chain those wrappers capture Empty. The actual rows then give

`C1=3; Ci=C_(i-1)+4=4i-1; Ei=Frame_fi[Fi,Empty]; Ni=4i+1`.

The new capture phases are legal under the earlier polarity assignment: data/continuations positive, controllers including Ret negative. Their replacements fit the four-node, arity-two bound. FV computes syntactic free names locally; Project pays for copying the actual environment and uses nearest Lookup. A host relevance oracle has not been hidden in these rows.

The four audited sources of D/constructor collisions sum to

`(2k²-k)+(2k²-k)+k+(2k²-k-1)=6k²-2k-1`, for k>=1.

Additional analysis, set, continuation, lookup, erase and neural work remain charged. On this particular family they preserve the O(k²) total-work upper bound. Retained environments are O(k); that is not a schedule-uniform peak-space theorem. Thus the repair really removes the earlier exponential behavior on this family. Calling it ineffective would discard a valid construction.

The new fixed-type thunk family establishes the consequential remaining limit. With `H=A→A`, `B=H→H→H`, both necessary predecessor values are captured in

`fi = λq:B. (q (λv:A. f_(i-1) q) (λw:A. f_(i-2) q)) (C a)`.

Both branch arguments become closures under call-by-value; the selector chooses one thunk before it is applied. The selected semantic call chain has O(k) depth. The thirteen-node body plus Closure, two Frames and Empty give

`C0=C1=3; Ci=C_(i-1)+C_(i-2)+17=20 Fib_(i+1)-17`.

I checked the constant and the fixed type order. The overlapping ancestry is inside relevant captured values, not irrelevant environment frames removable by perfect FV analysis. For the intermediate let environments the audit's `Ni=Ci+C_(i-1)+3` and final `Nk=Ck+2` follow from the remaining free predecessor. Full cleanup still pays for the generated owned copies; streaming alone does not insert fusion or sharing.

An immutable heap can keep each body/value once, link the predecessors, and build and follow the selected path with O(k) objects/work on this family. It needs neither semantic simplification nor an FV oracle. This is an execution comparison, not proof that an equally informed neural RAM learner acquires it at equal cost. Nor is it a lower bound on pair-local graph laws: those could acquire sharing, a reference discipline or a different representation. It closes only a general efficiency reading of the current owned-tree witness.

## The stronger example tests behavior, not a unique internal organization

The captured H/Church N example has the claimed distinctions: two N uses yield outer a, one yields b, and dynamic capture at the inner x yields c. Varying input Boolean, transformations, use count, names and surrounding composition is necessary because zero uses also yields a. The raw expansion and absence of a host NOT/BIND interface matter.

This is a better diagnostic than the old constant F. It does not require physical cloning: a valid interpreter can share, specialize or evaluate differently. Prospective replacements of a real acquired N interface by Identity, with a same-interface sham, could support a mechanism account. Redirecting an acquired capture link could support a binding account. Such interventions require real acquired functional interfaces and validated controls, not assignment of the hand witness's role names to latent states. None has happened.

## What the actual rivals do and do not cover

Neural GPU already learns raw symbolic algorithms with a shared local neural transition. NRAM already learns I/O-controlled memory/pointer operations on an addressed substrate, and NLI learns latent primitive semantics plus execution. These narrow the claim: raw input, terminal supervision, learned operations and exact reference manipulation are individually insufficient distinctions. They do not prove equal acquisition costs for the proposed physical interaction organization. An invented learner with the identical new emitter would be a tautological comparator, not historical coverage.

[DGCA's original model and experiment](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/203478/1/isal_a_00658.pdf) use a shared neural rule for state changes and node duplication/deletion; duplication inherits connectivity. The inspected experiment samples random rules and characterizes graph growth. It does not demonstrate terminal-task acquisition of an evaluator. [Self-Organising Digital Circuits](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02606v2) trains a shared topology-masked rule to configure local functions, using fixed physical wires; its random-wiring regime includes recurrent task residual feedback. Adding/pruning/rewiring is explicitly left open. Combining those two papers into an already-demonstrated version of K would misstate both. Their boundaries leave a legitimate constructive opening, not a novelty certificate.

Exact mutable communication remains a meaningful architectural difference. It can make the next activation's location and retained payload part of an executable state transition, rather than a fixed-grid routing decision. But conventional discrete RAM also has exact links, and learned recurrent controllers can reuse a frozen law. Neither coherence nor bounded local footprint explains an acquisition advantage over that strongest rival. Dense differentiable addressing costs can be compared honestly; they cannot be imposed on every conventional learner at inference by definition.

## Success counterfactual and ONE next action

If the complete v2 search learned a frozen law from raw I/O, passed genuinely withheld capture/composition cases and paid all formation/cleanup costs, that would establish acquired internal graph organization. It would be a worthwhile intermediate result and substantially exceed the handwritten witness. It would not, on those observations alone, establish that exact mutable communication changes a major limitation of learning computational mechanisms. The present proposal leaves that latter consequence as a general possibility rather than a specific causal prediction. A tiny rule-choice win, longer interpreter trace or another compiler repair would not resolve it.

The next single action is **finish and freeze the already authorized neutral formation construction, then compare its actual law with v2 and an actual RAM/Neural-GPU organization before implementation**. Its deliverable must contain one worked acquisition/transfer contrast: which reusable dependency or communication interface is formed from allowed feedback; which subsequent learning or execution decisions that interface removes; what changes when the interface must serve a new composition; and the concrete rival process and total cost under which a difference is predicted. A conjectural mechanism and a plausible prediction suffice for another serious constructive pass; a separation theorem or cheap experiment is not demanded. If the fresh construction merely reproduces coherent whole-law sampling plus ordinary local program edits, record that equivalence and do not promote it through a new name.

One possible bearing question is whether a formed local interface lets the **same learned rule** serve newly combined dependencies without searching new context-specific routing/control choices. Neural GPU's shared local transition and RAM's reusable pointer controller must be allowed the same opportunity. The proposed distinction must survive that comparison, rather than rely only on asymptotic locality or ordinary weight sharing. The construction may instead reveal a different consequential bias; it need not preserve lambda syntax, owned trees or the current witness. This protects a bold architectural attempt without spending the next cycle making a competent conventional closure interpreter.

No finite training scale is admitted now because no specific acquisition contrast has yet been selected; this is not rejection for lacking a cheap test. Preserve v2 and its valid derivations as reference, stop its repair/implementation allocation, and review the next actual construct on its merits. Neither a negative toy nor an unfinished independent artifact ends MAIN.
