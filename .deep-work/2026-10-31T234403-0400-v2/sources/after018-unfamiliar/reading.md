SOURCE summary (paraphrase): cells have one principal port and ordered auxiliary ports. Rules preserve free boundaries and are unambiguous per symbol pair. Proposition 1 obtains a one-step diamond because active pairs cannot share cells. Reduced nets exclude cuts and principal cycles. Principal nets may stand for symbols. A translation replaces symbols with principal nets and must simulate rules; Proposition 4 bounds step expansion using minimum/maximum per-rule translation lengths. The three-combinator system is universal for interaction systems. These are finite-symbol rewriting results, not a neural learning theorem.
