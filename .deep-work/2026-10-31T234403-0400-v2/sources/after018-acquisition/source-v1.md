## 1. Learning to Teach

Fan, Yang, Fei Tian, Tao Qin, Xiang-Yang Li and Tie-Yan Liu. *Learning to Teach*. arXiv:1805.03643v1, 2018.

Primary full body: https://arxiv.org/html/1805.03643

Entered sections 3.1-3.2 and 4.2-4.3: equation (1) represents a student trained from data, loss and hypothesis space; the teacher chooses those inputs and is optimized using student outcomes. Their instantiation learns training-data selection with policy gradients and state features from learning progress. This removes any novelty claim for a teacher conditioned on student state, an outcome-selected curriculum, or a teacher-student loop. V1's proposed additional constraint is that the child's teaching computation itself must be constructed and assessed through a further actual child. The present reading does not establish priority for that constraint.

## 2. DreamCoder

Ellis, Kevin, et al. *DreamCoder: Bootstrapping Inductive Program Synthesis with Wake-Sleep Library Learning*. PLDI 2021.

Primary PDF: https://people.csail.mit.edu/asolar/papers/EllisWNSMHCST21.pdf

Entered PDF pp. 3-4, sections 2.1 and 2.3; PDF p. 7, section 4, its MAP/posterior recognition objectives and fantasy generation. Library programs produce input/output problems used to train the neural recognition model. Consequently acquired computation generating subsequent learning material is already present. V1 cannot claim that general feedback loop. Its proposed object is instead an actual learner whose teaching ability is transmitted through learning and judged by later descendant task outcomes. A program library could implement such a constructor; the source's inspected objectives are not themselves an equal-resource comparison with that proposed constraint.

## 3. SEAL: own-generated data/directives cause weight updates

Zweiger, Adam, et al. *Self-Adapting Language Models*. arXiv:2506.10943v2, September 2025.

Primary full body: https://arxiv.org/html/2506.10943v2

Entered section 3.1, equations (1)-(4), Algorithm 1, and section 5. The model generates self-edits, SFT applies them, and post-update task outcomes train generation. The method uses on-policy evaluations because reward depends on the current parameters; its estimator stops gradients through reward. The limitations explicitly say its setup does not optimize retention. V1's differentiable finite-lesson instance and second-descendant objective differ from this inspected implementation. This does not establish novelty over multi-step meta-learning or a two-step SEAL extension. A same-information self-edit baseline that also scores subsequent teaching is the closest serious alternative, not a static network.

## 4. Self-replicating neural programs

Schmidgall, Samuel. *Self-Replicating Neural Programs*. arXiv:2109.12786v2, 2021.

Primary full body: https://arxiv.org/html/2109.12786

Entered section 2.1, especially the actual generation/training pipeline: the program stores its own instructions, trains an LSTM to generate them, and then writes/executes the generated program; section 2.2 was located. Reproduction of the program's training machinery is therefore a very close prior idea. V1 instead specifies a bounded in-memory lesson channel and external task outcomes through descendants; that representational distinction alone is insufficient significance. No source code or host execution from this paper was used. Its strong natural-selection/autonomy framing is not adopted as a result about V1.

## 5. SeRANN

Shvartzman, Boaz and Yoav Ram. *Self-replicating artificial neural networks give rise to universal evolutionary dynamics*. PLOS Computational Biology 20(3), e1012004, 2024.

Primary full article: https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012004

Entered the introduction's genotype/phenotype pipeline and Methods description. The replicated genotype encodes architecture and hyperparameters; network weights are learned afresh rather than inherited. The replication and classification tasks are distinct training components. Neural replication and externally selected neural development are therefore prior topics. V1 concerns transmission of an acquired teaching computation through its own lesson channel; it cannot claim that coupling development, selection and replication is new. No experimental numbers from this paper are used in the v1 argument.
