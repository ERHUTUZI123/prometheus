## R: where the source boundary is sharp

**ATTACK on v0, not v1.** If every trial observes G(R), then u=v accepted into R will hold by construction in that trial system. A witness against an empirical equation must come from a frozen teacher/action oracle independent of the candidate quotient. Even with such an oracle, agreement on tested states and continuations does not identify unrestricted semantics. Short finite presentations can agree to any fixed tested depth and diverge later.

**RECONSTRUCTION for v1.** A selected relation need not be a discovered fact. It can be an architectural choice, just as choosing a recurrent cell is a choice. Its consistency and executable semantics are obligations of the runtime; task usefulness is an empirical matter. This route survives the circularity objection. It asks whether an operational-law representation is a useful learnable organization of computation. Existing graphs, rewriting and allocation are allowed primitives. Their substantial computational contribution must remain visible.

The closest direct rival is therefore not only automata learning. It is architecture/program search in the same meta-language: an operational presentation is executable code for an abstract data resource. [Guttag's actual §§2–4](https://cecs.wright.edu/~tkprasad/courses/cs7100/guttag-cacm77.pdf) already uses relationships among operations to specify resources independently of representations. [DreamCoder's wake/abstraction/dreaming cycle](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.08381), pp.4–7, already grows callable libraries and trains neural search; abstraction includes bounded semantic refactorings, not just literal repeated subtrees. R must distinguish changing state equivalences/effect laws from adding another callable interpreter or library routine by a resource/accessibility consequence.

A fair control cannot deny the library learner dynamic allocation or parent pointers while giving R a free history graph. Conversely, it need not grant a hand-coded stack as the only baseline. Use both a same-runtime search control, which isolates neural proposal learning, and a same-effect program-library control, which tests the law representation. A win over enumeration alone establishes useful amortized search, not yet a new organizational principle.

**Fragile retention step, repaired explicitly in v1.** Adding equations to an existing resource can merge previously distinguishable states and change the behavior of old calls. R v1 therefore retains old rule modules and restricts extension with fresh root symbols; its stated intertwining condition preserves old finite compositions. An equation's shortness and success on today's task do not imply conservative extension. Keeping old versions prevents silent reinterpretation; it does not ensure an evolving controller can still reach or exploit them. Account for both operational preservation and later task retention.

## K: protect the real derivative; attack repeated absorption

**RECONSTRUCTION.** For Z(F)=sum_s product_v F_v(s_v), holding all but F_v fixed gives a linear function of F_v. Thus dZ/dF_v(a) is the contraction obtained by fixing its incident assignment a and removing that tensor. For a parameter changing several entries/tensors, apply the chain rule and sum the corresponding insertions. If each inserted tensor is counted as one more arbitrary degree-d exception, its contraction has at most d(k+1) conditioned edge bits. The per-insertion exponential factor is therefore at most 2^{d(k+1)}, times core evaluation and exact arithmetic cost. All-entry/all-vertex gradients need their additional count; no free full gradient is implied.

A pointwise compiled value cannot be differentiated as though it represented the ambient function in a neighbourhood. The insertion construction repairs this. It remains an elementary semantic derivative with a known special-purpose evaluator. [Liao et al., §II](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1903.09650) is the appropriate AD boundary, not evidence that this sparse acquisition loop was previously solved.

The unusually close residual-localization antecedent is [Mele–Herasymenko, Theorem 26](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18665): after moving Gaussian factors, the conjugated non-Gaussian generators occupy a bounded span; an orthogonal rotation places that span in a small coordinate subsystem. Their tomography algorithm learns the frame, then learns the small remaining state. This is stronger precedent than merely saying “Gaussian circuits are known.” It still does not implement K's neural semantic-learning loop or guarantee task acquisition under an exception budget.

K supplies the tractable algebra and its evaluator. Learning port bases and regions is therefore learning membership/decomposition in supplied execution structure; it should not be reported as discovery of a new algebra. That can still be scientifically important if it changes acquisition. Existing [Cai–Guo–Williams algorithms](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/hguo/papers/HA-general.pdf), Theorems 1.1–1.2 and §4, require strong deterministic recognition controls for their stated affine/product classes. Do not claim they solve every edge-dependent local-gauge matchgate problem.

**Strongest surviving objection:** a sparse update can leave the exact tractable variety, but generic continued updates accumulate exceptions. Exact recompilation cannot remove an irreducible semantic departure. A fixed tractable family closed under allowed gluing does not become more expressive merely by recognizing larger regions. The prospective gain must concern the trajectory through function/representation space: useful departures become cheaply absorbed while old callable behavior survives. The later construction repairs the absence of any explicit release route. For quartics P=γ1γ2γ3γ4 and Q=γ1γ2γ3γ5, P²=Q²=I, PQ=γ4γ5, and {P,Q}=0. With A=(I+iP)/√2, direct expansion gives AQA†=iPQ, hence A(cI+i sQ)A†=cI−sPQ. Since (PQ)²=−I and c²+s²=1, this is an entire Gaussian family, not a scalar coincidence. A rational Cayley parameterization handles c,s exactly; the field containing i and √2 must still be counted. Three temporary quartic gates can therefore become one differentiable Gaussian primitive, releasing the explicit defect count. This is constructive mechanism evidence, with an independent executable rebuild still pending at review time. It is also an identity route available to the deterministic compiler; no neural acquisition advantage follows. Useful repeated release beyond supplied identities remains open. Rejecting this solely for using a known core would be wrong; treating this designed family as evidence of general absorption would also be wrong.

**All-input certificate condition.** If F_v depends on x, equality of numerical boundary tables at a training x does not prove equality as functions of x. Either expose all x dependence through certified boundary ports, give a parameterized identity, or restrict the stated certificate to the fixed instance. The same distinction applies to retained gradient contracts.

## One productive repair, now convergent with R v1: typed resource extension

The reviewer’s constructive repair converges with the now-read R v1 rather than adding a separate invention: make a resource proposal an explicit tuple (new state sort, generators, oriented law set, observation/effect interface, cost cap). Runtime enforcement defines the new resource, and external task feedback selects it; no claim of empirical law truth is made. Freeze every already installed resource. Permit new resources and adapters to call old versions, but require the old-state transition of each adapter to match its declared contract. The simplest safe extension leaves unrelated old state exactly unchanged, checked by the executable wrapper. This separates creation of new memory organization from destruction of old semantics without demanding a general conservative-extension theorem.

This is a mechanism repair, not itself a novelty claim. Its substantive question is whether learning short effect relations exposes a reusable organization that substantially eases later capability acquisition under the same primitives and complete cost accounting. A particularly discriminating case would require a held-out combination of effects for which a fixed memory catalogue is inadequate, while the program-library rival still has enough primitives to implement it. The target is a learning/resource consequence, not an impossible representability separation. Define that concrete consequence and control before a major implementation; an unsuccessful result can close the proposed mechanism without disproving the broader mission.

**Disposition boundary:** R v1 is directly recognizable as neural-guided synthesis of executable typed rewrite programs, but no source here proves full coverage of its proposed learning architecture. Its substantive acquisition distinction is presently missing, rather than decisively refuted. K has a concrete release family and valid local-gradient route, but those remain available to its named deterministic rival. These are reasons to sharpen the scientific claim before major training, not broad novelty vetoes.
