## Exact state and protected map

For an acquired target area a and ONE shared learned shape coordinate rho, let

    w1=a rho,                       w2=a(1-rho),
    D²=a²/4+(1-a²)/(12 rho(1-rho)),
    mu1=1/2-(1-rho)D,               mu2=1/2+rho D,
    q=(mu1-w1/2, mu1+w1/2, mu2-w2/2, mu2+w2/2).

The two intervals have total length a. Their normalized first and second moments are

    mean(x)=rho mu1+(1-rho)mu2=1/2,
    mean(x²)=1/4+rho(1-rho)D²
                    +a²[rho³+(1-rho)³]/12=1/3.

Therefore, for P1=x(1-x), P2=x(1-x)(2x-1),

    sum_j [P1(q2j)-P1(q2j-1)]=0,
    sum_j [P2(q2j)-P2(q2j-1)]=0.

This follows by integrating P1'=1-2x and P2'=-6x²+6x-1 over the intervals. The ideal geometric area is exactly a for every response X=x+c1P1+c2P2, Y=y, without estimating c1 or c2. The protected boundaries exclude a free unknown linear scaling of the signal. If X=(1+e)x+..., the remaining area error is e*a, so nonzero-area protection fails for that enlarged class. That is a change in the physical premise, not an unfair disadvantage to a competitor on the same plant.

For a in [.1,.4], rho in [.45,.55], the chart is smooth and uniformly ordered. Conservative bounds .56<D<.58 give first/last boundary margin >.071, interval widths >=.045 and central gap >.36. The moment-map derivative has rows s_i times 1, 1-2q_i, -6q_i²+6q_i-1, where s=(-,+,-,+). Four distinct q_i give rank3 by the degree-two Vandermonde property. Thus the one-dimensional fiber is actual shape freedom, rather than a neural-coordinate redundancy. At rho=.5 its tangent is proportional to (D-a/2,D+a/2,D+a/2,D-a/2), nonzero throughout the interval.

A possible shared neural chart is rho_theta(a)=.5+.05*tanh(theta0+theta1*a). Every old acquired angle remains geometrically fixed under ALL changes of these weights. This exact algebraic statement does not imply a finite-time norm-error, deadline or action guarantee. It also does not show an advantage over the same chart used by an ordinary compiler.

Task inputs are independently prepared copies of Rz(beta*)|+>, measured in X/Y. Counts determine a retained target interval B; no target angle/waveform is passed to the learner. For the static-parasitic echo construction a=beta_hat/pi; this lies inside the chart for the proposed task range. New measured task information is what supplies a new operation, while the robust contour constructor and old logical X are supplied reusable tools.

Shared state includes shape/timing/nominal compensation parameters, calibration uncertainty U, task intervals and scalar operation programs. At each stage, choose shared parameters using the new operation's finite modeled loss/work under U; reject parameters violating previously retained actual operation contracts. The shape chart preserves all ideal areas uniformly and avoids old per-context KKT solves. Finite-execution checking and calibration may still scale with retained contexts. Do not hide that cost or freeze old heads while claiming shared adaptation.

## The consequential diagnostic, before a large implementation

The still unknown quantity is the actual safe shared-shape/timing/compensation set when the controller knows the plant only through paid Born observations. A finite-Hamiltonian diagnostic can determine whether this set remains useful during three new-operation acquisitions, whether it shrinks after adding old contracts, and what calibration/certification/execution work buys it. It must include published UGD/formula control, a legal direct idle pulse (and stronger composites if a favorable claim would depend on excluding them), and frozen/modular controls with equal calibration reuse. If a simpler legal controller dominates, no further expensive baseline is needed to reject an advantage claim for this pilot.

Use the existing six-mode model with fixed total parity and common idle code. Preserve orientation explicitly: the positive-octant path Delta3->Delta1->Delta2->Delta3 with the written Hcd convention produces exp(-pi*gamma1*gamma2/4); reverse it to implement the positive exchange. This sign is derived from the ordered three rotors, not learned by inspecting a hidden target. Check it before claiming new gate acquisition.
