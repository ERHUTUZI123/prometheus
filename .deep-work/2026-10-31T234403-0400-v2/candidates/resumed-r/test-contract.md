## Question and non-question

Can feedback-selected, recursively constructed cancellation programs become useful update actions on later tasks at lower **total** acquisition/adaptation cost than same-information parameterized options and a primitive-model planner? Demonstrating (8) from supplied equations is only a code/derivation check. The main test must discover which action interaction matters, learn its demand-to-argument law from observations and reuse the program on held-out state/demand contexts and a composed forward task.

The fixed local plasticity interface is a substantive assumption. Include a direct-owner-write control: if w is writable, one write solves the scalar task. Do not advertise the experiment as an advantage over unrestricted neural optimization. Do not claim alias-topology transfer or topology creation.

## Environment hidden from the learner

Use a small family of forward feature modules with persistent state (x,y,z,w) and two locally actuated signed shear controls. In canonical coordinates,

    X(a): x <- x+a
    Y(b): y <- y+b; z <- z+b*(alpha*x+beta);
          w <- w+b*(gamma*x*x/2+delta*x+epsilon).

Signs/scales/labels of the two exposed action handles and nonzero alpha,gamma vary with a frozen random seed. The learner gets a callable action interface, current forward probe outputs, target/protected demands, action bounds and inverse handles. It does not receive the polynomial equations, coefficients, canonical operator names, useful word or abc response law. All physical intermediate transitions are logged for later inspection; any learner access to state or transition features must be identical for rivals. Hidden equations live in the source for reproducibility and are therefore not a security-isolated holdout; no claim of model-blind isolation.

Protected outputs probe the x,y,z feature responses; target probes w. This deliberately supplies a simple meaningful computational effect. Final probes use fixed nonlinear input features so success is judged through an executed forward module as well as its retained weights. The linear independence of probes must be checked, not assumed from feature names.

Include three controls before interpreting any positive result: (i) commuting/flat fields, where no higher-order effect should be invented; (ii) a cubic/quartic perturbation or nonlinear f(x), where the old clean-effect law need not transfer and the macro should fail its contract, recalibrate or invoke a charged corrector; (iii) richer-interface direct write. No failure is silently removed.

## Methods with identical rights

Options: ordinary warm-started shooting/CEM over arbitrary signed primitive words up to the same expanded horizon, initialized with previously successful words and primitive options. It may retain a successful word with a learned goal/context-to-parameter map; compose/reuse options; fit repeated/inverse parameter ties when supported by its samples. Same library size, snapshots, probes and total primitive action allowance as R. It is not reset to random search on every task. The implementation must specify the word distribution, amplitude optimizer, archive rule and warm-start rule before execution. All internal model rollout counts and fitting/optimization CPU time reported. This is an independently justified strong method in this environment; a favorable R-vs-random-word result with model-options tying is no broad acquisition finding.

Direct-write: no claim of same actuation interface; report its one-write solution and zero protected drift. It exposes the restriction rather than serving as the purported weak rival.

## Outcomes and stopping

Primary finite outcome: cumulative paid primitive actions and actual oracle evaluations to a specified success threshold, including all acquisition, modeling and promotion work; failures remain censored at the common cap. Secondary: held-out task success, protected drift, forward-function error, total wall time and amortization break-even as future tasks accumulate. Report per-seed paired outcomes, not only a pooled mean.

A positive mechanism audit requires that a non-prelisted useful word enters the archive through task feedback; a learned argument law passes unseen demands/contexts; a nested retained word is actually reused; and the held-out forward effect is correct. A resource advantage additionally requires beating both fair rivals in total cost, with uncertainty stated. Neither establishes novelty or general usefulness.
