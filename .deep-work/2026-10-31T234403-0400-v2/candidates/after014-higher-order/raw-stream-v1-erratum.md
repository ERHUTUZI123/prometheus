# Clarifications and limitations of the preserved raw-stream v1

V1 is preserved. No experiment has been run. This note records issues raised by root and the supporting source/audit worker; it does not retroactively label the hand witness acquired.

1. **Polarity convention.** Tok, End, syntax/data constructors, Frame, Closure, Pair, Await frames, PFinish, KArg, KApply and Done have positive principals. Start, P, ReadL/V/C, **PReturn**, PCheckEnd, Eval, **Ret**, Lookup, UnpackArg, Apply, D and Erase have negative principals. Ret/PReturn are controllers that meet positive continuations. Every displayed v1 table row uses this same convention. A global inversion is equivalent; a partial role inversion would require new rules. The supporting worker manually reconstructed the port equations and found no invalid row.
2. **Scope trace versus acquired behavior.** The duplicated F in v1 is `lambda y.x`, a constant/idempotent function. The handwritten trace really constructs and uses two copies, but its final atom a does not extensionally require two calls: replacing `F(F b)` by `F b` gives the same answer. This is not a strong acquisition test for repeated function use. Acquisition-v2 therefore considers non-idempotent Church NOT, odd/even use counts, both Boolean inputs and captured readout scopes. Even those labels require mechanism interventions to establish actual copying.
3. **Terminal observation/cleanup.** Ret/Done can expose the output atom while independent D/Erase interactions remain. Future comparison must either drain the whole graph under a declared schedule, counting all cleanup and residual allocations, or declare a lazy-result protocol and charge retained garbage plus a stated subsequent cleanup policy. V2 chooses complete draining for its finite fitness definition. A correct output appearing early is not free completed computation.
4. **Owned-environment growth.** Root's actual `root-environment-growth.md` derives an O(k)-source family with `N_i=2N_(i-1)+3=4*2^i-3` owned environment nodes and exponential total copying. The v1 tree witness is therefore not evidence for an efficiency advantage over ordinary shared immutable heaps. Acquisition-v2 constructs a selective-capture repair and states its remaining sharing limitations. More fuel is not a repair.
5. **Source provenance.** V1's parser/evaluator/copy equations are hand construction. The later original Sinot token-passing reading identifies close execution precedent. Conventional Neural GPU/NRAM source records and the supporting manual audit are independent supporting artifacts; they are not experimental verification or proof of a novel acquisition principle.
