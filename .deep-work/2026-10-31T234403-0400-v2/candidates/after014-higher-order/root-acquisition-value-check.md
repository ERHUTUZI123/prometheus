# Success counterfactual after actual v2 construction

Root strategic judgment, not a theorem of impossibility or a novelty verdict. Read the complete v2, actual cost audit, and the checked conventional source bodies before writing this note. No training/experiment. This record supports review017, rather than presupposing its decision.

## What a complete v2 success would actually establish

If the described finite terminal-only search acquired a reusable local law from raw strings, froze it, and passed genuinely withheld scope/composition cases under full cost, it would establish an acquired graph-based algorithm/evaluator on that domain. This would be substantially more than the handwritten witness. Exact mutable communication is a real organization choice; an identical-K comparator or universal NN simulation cannot erase an empirical acquisition consequence.

However, the current complete construction does not yet supply a specific reason that this organization reduces an important learning restriction relative to an actual conventional learner. Coherent parameter exploration and neural behavioral patching are available to such learners. Neural GPU already learns raw local transition algorithms; NRAM learns pointer/memory control; NLI learns primitive semantics and recurrent execution. An acquired parser/closure discipline is a valuable intermediate target, not automatically a foundational principle. The lambda program family is a diagnostic, not the permanent endpoint.

## What remains different, and what comparison would mean

The candidate constrains every executed transition to one principal pair and gives its output exact boundary links. This can enforce name transport and noninterfering local execution while allowing learned graph growth. It may change the prior over algorithms and reduce irrelevant routing/scheduling choices. Those are meaningful hypotheses. The inspected work does not give a quantitative acquisition prediction or an identified class in which this prior pays its whole formation cost. A completed normalizing trace's schedule invariance is known interaction-net theory; the stochastic estimator and law-coherence improvement do not add task information.

A graph representation can avoid fixed-grid routing overhead, but conventional discrete RAM has exact mutable references too. NRAM's dense differentiable training overhead is relevant, yet an honest strong comparison cannot pretend all ordinary learning must retain dense fuzzy addressing at inference or cannot use parameter-space exploration. Conversely a handwritten heap evaluator has no extra task truth, but it does have supplied algorithmic prior: its execution bound is not an equal-acquisition-cost proof.

No immediate tiny test resolves this causal claim. Running the hand port rules, fitting a supplied few-row table, checking global name permutation, measuring a shared-law p^R illustration or adding longer terms would answer weaker questions already distinguished. A serious acquisition comparison must include raw formation, unknown scopes, non-idempotent use, withheld compositions, representation/cleanup work and failed search. Its nominal and label interfaces must match. These constraints are scientific, not a demand for a cheap verifier or complete proof before an unfinished architecture can proceed.

## What the actual repairs and attacks changed

V1's linear-input family had exponential owned environments. V2's actual local FV/Project protocol removes that failure on the checked chain, at quadratic rather than linear work. C's new fixed-type thunk family shows exponential owned captured-value ancestry even after exact FV projection, whereas ordinary heap sharing builds and follows a linear selected path. Thus the present target implementation cannot be the source of a broad resource advantage. It can remain a learning target, but further capture/compiler repair alone would drift into established implementation engineering.

Adding a persistent heap/read-only object interface could remove tree unfolding. That is a substantive representation change, not a small repair, and heap/NRAM mechanisms would then explain the sharing. It would still leave the need to identify and acquire an important organization bias. The active question should not silently become 'make a competent closure interpreter.'

## Decision interface

Protect the high-risk architecture from a false prior-coverage veto, while requiring a concrete consequence before allocating a large implementation. Current v2 is a serious explicit unexecuted construction; its learning advantage is OPEN. Root requests review017 to decide whether a finite formation study can test an important residual mechanism, or whether to reframe/pivot the specific version. The fresh independent constructive context may offer a better causal formulation; its unseen work is not evidence. No mathematical difficulty or lack of an immediate cheap experiment is itself a reason to declare the overall direction impossible.
