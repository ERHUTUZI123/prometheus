## Completed exact certificate-class frontier

For monotone sweeps, phi_lambda'(s)>=0. Any admissible retiming satisfies

    dt/ds >= max{1/S, phi_lambda'(s)/b}.

Integrate over[0,1]. Equality is attained by s_dot=min{S,b/phi'}, taking s_dot=S on zero-slope intervals. Piecewise switching is permitted by this declared clock model; impose acceleration/clock-generation limits separately if real hardware requires them. Therefore

    T_cert(lambda;S,b)
      =(1-epsilon) max{1/S,2pi lambda/b}
       +epsilon max{1/S,2pi[lambda+(1-lambda)/epsilon]/b}.

This is an exact minimum WITHIN the sufficient tube-certificate class, not a globally optimal nonlinear q-control theorem. In particular

    T_cert(1)=max{1/S,2pi/b},
    T_cert(0)=(1-epsilon)/S+max{epsilon/S,2pi/b}.

The formulas were derived independently in root's reasoning for the endpoints and supplied generally by the actual supervisor; this record integrates/checks the proof rather than claiming an untouched blind replication. For the original strict rate threshold without a positive slack, describe its boundary as an infimum unless a separate non-strict invariant-set argument is invoked.

## What this changes in the cost claim

When input-clock retiming is unrestricted, the plateau penalty tends to zero as S grows. The fixed initial map can execute the same physical force waveform as the learned uniform map, using a compiled clock schedule. Its lookup/inverse is piecewise linear in this family; no expensive generic neural inverse is required. Skipping a chart plateau need not move the physical q or change the applied force. An internal chart-rate restriction cannot be invented as a physical cost merely to exclude this alternative.

With a genuinely limited or externally fixed clock, spreading phase can instead change a real available deadline frontier. That distinction must be prospectively justified by the task/control interface, not selected after observing a win. The old K failed-deadline/successful-deadline proof remains correct for its explicitly fixed linear sweep; it never established a retiming-independent resource advantage.

An ordinary controller can also choose lambda=1 immediately. Thus eight analytic gradient steps are not an acquisition-cost advantage in this fully known one-weight family. Their value is a controlled existence witness, not evidence that the slow initial geometry was hard to discover or typical. No favorable or adverse general topology claim follows.

A complete workload comparison must include

    C_total = C_discovery + C_training + C_certificate/refresh
            + C_storage/routing + C_clock + C_preparation/migration
            + N_calls*C_actual_execution,

or report the separate vector when weights are not physically specified. Faster T alone is insufficient. If learned geometry costs A once and saves Delta per call over a matched retimer, a break-even inequality is N_calls*Delta>A; neither Delta nor hardware cost weights have been measured here. Frozen controllers/new modules and direct waveform control remain legal strategies.
