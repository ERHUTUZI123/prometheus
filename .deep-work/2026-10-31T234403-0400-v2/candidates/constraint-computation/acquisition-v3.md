## Judgment and bearing gap

Three different causal achievements must be separated: (a) identifying an externally existing continuous relation from observations; (b) constructing or changing the learner's own internal relation in response to task feedback; (c) finding and using operations supported by that relation's continuation. A local relation fit followed by a classical monodromy compiler supplies a concrete route to (a,c). It does not, by itself, establish autonomous internal construction (b). The first proposal below is an explicit acquisition scaffold and a useful rival; the repair must make the internal structural choice task-driven.

The scientific prospect is that continuous evidence can train a reusable internal relation, while its global path lifts furnish discrete operations that were never individually supervised. Protected continuation corridors would retain acquired operations during later relation changes. The strong alternative is ordinary algebraic/model identification plus a paid continuation planner and finite-state controller. No advantage over that alternative is established.

## First construct: evidence-to-operator pipeline, without supplied gate labels

Use a structured holomorphic relation, not an arbitrary real residual MLP. A polynomial network computes

    F_theta(z,u) = z^3 + a z^2 + b z + c + u(d z + e).

The leading coefficient is fixed to one, preventing zero-function collapse. Coefficients are trainable complex weights (two real coordinates each); gradients or direct least squares are legitimate alternatives. Degree-two and degree-three model proposals are compared with all proposals charged. Monomial/product units and a continuation solver are designed primitives. No claim is made that these primitives emerged.

The data interface supplies shuffled continuous pairs (z,u) on independently observed local relation arcs; it does not supply critical values, paths, branch identities between disconnected arcs, loop labels, or desired permutations. Data collection, preparations/resets, local perturbations, noise and precision must be counted. Five generic complex pairs identify the five free coefficients of this specific family when the complex design matrix with rows [z^2,z,1,uz,u] has full rank. This is a finite-dimensional prior, not a consequence of arbitrary finite samples. Rank conditioning and held-out fit are required; pointwise fit alone is not a corridor certificate.

After fitting, the learner computes the discriminant/critical points of its own fitted relation, constructs bounded candidate handles and isolating loops, and transports every registered resting branch through them. Every model evaluation, derivative, critical solve, attempted handle, adaptive tracking step, precision increase and rejected proposal is paid. A desired branch permutation is never passed to this compiler. The observed endpoint permutation is an output of its tracked execution. A classical fitted-model continuation compiler receives exactly the same information and abilities.

At a regular resting control the retained state is the actual z, not a freshly chosen root. Execution uses

    z_dot = -[D_z F_theta]^{-1}(D_u F_theta u_dot + D_theta F_theta theta_dot + kappa F_theta).

This is real-coordinate notation for two complex residual coordinates. Nonsingularity is checked, finite correction is charged, and any tracking failure rejects the proposed operation. Parameter migration also moves decoder anchors; numeric root locations need not remain fixed. Stored loops are composable by concatenation from the same resting control, with z carried through the whole word. An FSM may cache the measured permutation, but may not silently receive unobserved ones for free.

For the already proved family F=(z-r)(z^2-u)-epsilon, the learned coefficients would be a=-r, b=0, c=-epsilon, d=-1, e=r. These values are an explanatory specialization, not learner input. Old A is preserved only if the actual coefficient migration and entire old path family have a certificate such as the existing Rouché bound. The fitted monic-cubic family alone does not provide this certificate.

ATTACK: This pipeline can infer an externally supplied cubic's topology and execute it, but a hand-designed target relation has supplied its essential computational organization. Calling the fitted coefficients an internal neural mechanism does not establish task-driven construction. A repaired mechanism must choose an internal growth mutation, validate preservation, and select a useful resulting operation from task feedback. That is the remaining constructive task in this artifact.

## Epistemic boundary

The prior executed check already establishes safe selected old-gate retention and a new noncommuting B for manually constructed relations/paths in its stated regime. It did not train these coefficients or acquire B from feedback. No completed growth test should be rerun to support the present acquisition claim. Finite unrestricted relation samples cannot determine unobserved global topology; an admissible model class plus uncertainty/corridor control is a genuine assumption, and its cost belongs in comparisons.

## Constructive repair: reward-selected internal sheet growth

This is the first repaired v3 construct; the later degree-four operational-library refinement is the proposed central test. It is an explicit topology-structured program search, not an unexplained meta-controller or a renamed form of Laws A/B. It closes the causal construction gap in a limited operational sense: task feedback selects an actual change to the internal relation and its executable control path. It does not establish a resource or acquisition advantage over the same symbolic constructor.

### State, permissions, and task interface

The learner owns an internal relation P, current transported state z, resting control u0=1, a finite operation library whose entries are actual control paths, a decoder-anchor set transported with the relation, and accumulated proposal/reward/work records. Its initial operation A is the known old exchange on P=z^2-u. The mechanism may already have learned P from relation data; that learning is not assumed to supply any new gate. All finite polynomial coefficients, including any trainable network weights that produce them, are explicit inspectable state.

A new task supplies episodic terminal scalar reward R(z_end,context), with no teacher-supplied relation coefficients, critical points, handles, loop, gate name or permutation. For the worked case the reward has support near an as-yet unreachable home-state output around 3, while old A episodes must remain valid. A task reward necessarily conveys task information; hiding this under the word unsupervised would be misleading. New permutation labels are not separately provided to the compiler. The task evaluator must be outside the learner and inaccessible except by the declared reward queries.

The designed structural grammar permits one new factor and coupling,

    Q_(r,epsilon)(z,u)=(z-r)P(z,u)-epsilon,
    r in [5/2,7/2], 0<=epsilon<=1/5.

The interval, positive coupling range, one-factor mutation family, monomial computation and continuation solver are supplied inductive biases. The learner selects the particular r, nonzero epsilon and executable path from task feedback. It is not discovering a wholly unrestricted architecture. A bounded grid or bounded sampled proposal set makes this law fully executable; continuous fitting can subsequently refine r and epsilon within the certificate. A neural policy is optional and would have to earn a consequence over direct search.

The structural acceptance score is observed task improvement less a declared construction/execution penalty. Reject a candidate if old-corridor certification fails, any required continuation fails, or its measured gain does not pay the declared cost. The penalty must not be chosen after seeing results. A bounded proposal ordering supplies the entire search controller; no semantic answer is delegated to an unimplemented planner.

### One update cycle, including honest rollback

1. Select (r,epsilon) from the fixed proposal distribution/order. In the first finite version use r in {5/2,3,7/2} and epsilon in {1/20,1/5}; the no-coupling control uses epsilon=0. All six candidates and their ordering are declared before evaluation. Proposing r=3 is one member of a generic bounded list, not a teacher-selected successful relation.
2. Append the initially disconnected branch r. At epsilon=0, every old state already satisfies Q=0. Migrate the actual retained z and all registered decoder anchors through coefficient homotopy s epsilon, 0<=s<=1, using the full parameter derivative. Appending a branch is an explicit structural operation; it is not a state reset or an evaluation oracle.
3. Compute critical points and critical control values of the proposed internal Q. Generate an entire bounded candidate path family from these values without task-specific path labels. A concrete compiler chooses an isolating radius smaller than one fifth of the nearest distinct critical-value separation; it tries straight handles first, then a fixed finite list of polygonal detours if needed. Every handle must avoid the certified exclusion disks and pass actual continuation checks. Enumerate both loop orientations. Reject if no declared handle works; do not silently invent an oracle path.
4. Lift every proposed loop from every registered resting branch, carrying z. Its measured endpoint correspondence is a compiler output. Evaluate task reward only on actually executed episodes. A path reaches a new state only if the old branch is continuously carried there; choosing a newly solved root is forbidden. Rank candidates by the frozen score. Loop/path evaluation and task queries are both charged, even when internal model prediction could cheaply screen a proposal.
5. If a trial loop is rejected, traverse its exact reversed control path to undo its branch action. Then reverse the coefficient homotopy. The old state returns through continuation; it is not reseeded to a desired root. Discard the disconnected added branch only after the actual state is back on an old component. If numerical reversal fails, stop/reject with an honest execution failure. A different home-branch task episode needs a declared preparation path; it is not a free reset.
6. Accept the winning relation, its actual path, its carried-state anchors and its preservation certificate. The retained operation library is old A plus discovered B and their reverse paths. New tasks may concatenate them. Store the measured branch action as a cache if useful, but count that cache and do not replace the continuous actuator by it when the task requires actual path execution. Before any later coefficient learning/growth, add B's entire actual corridor to the protected family and obtain its own boundary/separation certificate. The old A bound does not automatically protect B. A numerical minimum sampled along B is evidence for tracking, not a uniform analytic certificate for future updates; if that new certificate is absent, later B retention remains unverified.

No reward gradient through the discrete permutation is assumed. A finite structural event crosses from disconnected epsilon=0 to a coupled relation; reward selects this event and a loop. Within a fixed class, smooth training may refine numerical readout, margins or execution cost, but the old gradient obstruction remains true.

### Uniform preservation certificate for the actual proposal family

For the same declared old-path annulus .95<=|u|<=1.05, old roots satisfy .97<|sqrt(u)|<1.03. Radius-1/4 disks around them are disjoint. For every r in [2.5,3.5], on each old-root disk boundary,

    |(z-r)(z^2-u)| >= (1/4)(2*.97-.25)(2.5-1.03-.25)
                         = .51545 > 1/5.

On the radius-1/4 disk around r,

    |(z-r)(z^2-u)| >= (1/4)[(2.5-.25)^2-1.05]
                         = 1.003125 > 1/5.

These are coarse sufficient bounds. Rouché therefore gives one root per moving disk throughout every coupling homotopy, all declared old-path deformations, and continuous changes of r inside the interval. Old A remains its old exchange while the added branch is fixed by A. This preserves semantic branch action, not numerical root coordinates. The certificate supplies an exact old-operation constraint over the entire declared family, without relying on finitely sampled old task rewards. Numerical continuation still has its own finite-error checks and can fail.

This is the particular prospective organizational benefit: structural exploration changes a shared continuous relation and moves all its root coordinates, while preserving an old global operation by a geometric invariant. A table can preserve old transitions by retaining them explicitly; the present construction gives another retention mechanism, not an established superiority over that table.

### Complete worked process, analytical illustration rather than new execution

Start at u=1,z=+1 in P=z^2-u; A carries it to -1 and a second A returns it to +1. Let the new task reward be one for a terminal home-control state in |z-3|<1/10 and zero otherwise. The learner sees the scalar result of a query, not this formula. This deliberately simple task makes the causal construction inspectable; it is not evidence of a hard new capability.

Suppose the declared order tests r=2.5,epsilon=.05 first. Its new resting root lies near 2.5+.05/(2.5^2-1), outside the rewarded region. The compiler may discover a path to it, but task feedback rejects that relation/path pair. Reversing that path and the coefficient homotopy restores the carried old state before the next trial.

For r=3,epsilon=.05, the internal relation is Q=(z-3)(z^2-u)-.05. The compiler derives, rather than receives,

    Q_z=3z^2-6z-u,
    Q=Q_z=0  =>  2z(z-3)^2+.05=0,
    u_critical=3z^2-6z.

The old critical value and the two new critical values are obtained by the paid solve. The new values are near 9 +/- 2i sqrt(.3). The compiler enumerates isolating loops/handles from its derived values and tracks them, rather than hard-coding a loop centered on those explanatory approximations. The existing growth evidence already checks one admissible such path for this relation. A fresh acquisition test would check the compiler's independently generated candidates, not reuse that successful path as learner input.

For an actual candidate exchanging the positive old root with the new one, the endpoint is the new resting root. Write it as 3+delta. Then

    delta(8+6delta+delta^2)=.05.

The left side is increasing for delta>=0, is zero at zero, and exceeds .05 at delta=.00625. Hence 0<delta<.00625, giving positive reward. The learner accepts the actual transported path B on its observed gain, subject to the work penalty. The mathematical example shows existence of a rewarded candidate, not that every bounded compiler will discover it. Compiler completeness and finite numerical success remain testable obligations.

The no-coupling candidate epsilon=0 contains a stored root at 3 but its old P component is disconnected from it. Starting from the old branch and using only valid continuation away from intersections cannot reach the new component. A method that reports success there has reset/reseeded or changed its execution semantics. This control distinguishes adding an output location from constructing an executable coupling.

Once B is acquired, the carried-state library has A=(old negative,old positive) and B=(old positive,new). Its actual lifts therefore predict AA=BB=identity and AB!=BA on the three registered states. All held-out words are executed by continuous concatenation and compared only afterward with the predicted composition. For this convention, starting from the old positive branch, A then B ends on the old negative branch, whereas B then A ends on the new branch. No independent root choice is allowed between letters. A useful task beyond this illustration must require such an interaction; reaching a third output alone is too weak a scientific endpoint.

## Neural learning and the continuous-evidence extension

The finite law above supplies autonomous internal structural selection, but contains no necessary neural component. Making its proposal distribution a neural network would not repair that evidential gap. The initial evidence-to-operator learner is useful as a distinct acquisition route: continuous local data can train a structured polynomial network, and its learned continuation can induce unsupervised operations. It should not be silently substituted for the task-driven construction test.

A concrete combined learner may have theta=(r,epsilon,delta-coefficients), a polynomial-network relation Q_theta=(z-r)P-epsilon+Delta_theta(z,u), and ordinary gradient steps on continuous relation/readout evidence. Use fixed monic leading coefficient and keep every update inside a certified bound

    sup_(u in old paths, z on protected disk boundaries) |Delta_theta(z,u)|
        < old boundary margin - |epsilon|.

For a finite polynomial expansion Delta_theta=sum theta_j z^p_j u^q_j, the sufficient bound sum |theta_j| M_z^p_j M_u^q_j is computable on a bounded old corridor; interval/rational bounds may improve it. The common root disks must remain disjoint and the full homotopy must satisfy the strict inequality. For a subsequently acquired B, replace the old corridor by the union of all currently protected generator corridors and use an actual lower bound of the current relation on every disk boundary. No certificate for this larger family has yet been constructed here. Projecting updates into a certified conservative coefficient ball is a completely specified retention rule conditional on obtaining that bound. It can be restrictive, and if it prevents the new data from being fit, the correct result is failure rather than claiming arbitrary neural plasticity.

Continuous fitting therefore tunes genuinely shared coefficients under a global-operation constraint. Structural proposals add a new factor when the current admitted class has failed the fixed task objective; task score chooses whether to retain it. The representation does not receive desired gate permutations, and the compiler still pays to discover the resulting loops. However, a symbolic constrained polynomial/rational learner can implement these same updates. An acquisition advantage needs a further concrete consequence, such as lower total sample/compile cost or a useful larger family of shared edits, not the presence of trainable weights alone.

The learned-coefficient extension also has an exact obstruction. Given finitely many observations (z_i,u_i), a nonzero polynomial H(z,u) that vanishes at all of them can be added as lambda H. On unseen corridors it can become large, create critical points or change the finite-fiber structure while leaving all observed residuals unchanged. Thus even zero training error and a model consensus do not identify global topology without the stated degree/regularity/corridor prior. Extra data reduce uncertainty within a declared class; they cannot make that obstruction disappear for unrestricted MLPs.

## Strong rivals, access and accounting

1. **Same-information symbolic growth plus continuation.** It receives the same P, task rewards, candidate grammar/order, certificates, precision, critical-point solver and handle planner. It carries the same physical/virtual z and pays the same work. It can reproduce the complete construction exactly. This is the central algorithmic rival and an equality baseline, not a generic neural-network simulability argument.
2. **Learned continuous model plus continuation.** On the relation-evidence arm it gets the same samples, derivative/preparation access and noise model, fits the same monic family by direct least squares or rational identification, and pays for model order selection, certification and compilation. Two-parameter fitting is an additional strong special-case baseline, not evidence the neural learner inferred a broad relation family. On internally owned Q, there is no hidden coefficient-identification cost: the constructor already knows the coefficients it wrote.
3. **Growing finite-state library.** If the task only reads symbolic home-state outputs, this rival may add an output state and install the rewarded transition without integrating roots. Give it the same rewards and charge its search, storage and old-transition protections. It may be far cheaper. If actual continuous actuation is part of the task, it can cache the same learned path/permutation but must execute the path; adding a free endpoint assignment would change the actuator interface. Report these two interfaces separately.
4. **Fixed relation / no growth** and **new branch / no coupling.** The first tests whether a new state is needed under this actuator; the second tests whether branch addition alone explains reward. Do not call their inferiority superiority over ordinary computation.

Count all task queries, preparations and rollback episodes, parameter proposals, model-fitting passes, critical solves including failed/duplicate ones, path candidates, tracked starting branches, predictor/corrector evaluations, adaptive halvings, precision changes, certificate work, retained coefficients/paths/caches and held-out execution. A cheap stored loop after expensive search is not a cheap acquisition result. The given substrate is digital simulation, so no hardware energy or physical topological-noise advantage follows.

## Stronger acquisition target: learned operation library, not a coefficient sweep

The six-candidate r/epsilon search above is retained as the first completely specified construction scaffold. It is too close to repeating the completed growth calculation to serve as the central new result. The more useful acquired object is the operational library: a generic constructor proposes all admissible local loops, actual transport learns their effects, task feedback selects a useful compositional program, and subsequent unseen programs execute through the same retained state. A single designed growth mutation can be offered and accepted or rejected; its coefficients need not be spuriously called learned.

Root's `root-compositional-acquisition.md` supplies two relevant standard group-action reconstructions. A common transported embedding that intertwines every old generator intertwines every finite old word by induction. If the old group is transitive, any discovered transposition between an old state and the new state generates the full enlarged symmetric group together with the old library. These statements do not imply efficient word synthesis or indefinitely reliable numerical execution.

One new test can avoid the previously completed cubic witness entirely. Begin with

    P=z^3-u,
    Q=(z-r)(z^3-u)-epsilon,
    r in [2.5,3.5], 0<epsilon<=.2.

The old unit control loop is a three-cycle A on the three old roots. Propose one declared nonzero coupling rather than sweeping the old r/epsilon cases. All degree-four coefficients are owned and available to both the learner and the conventional planner. The actual new paths and their effects are not supplied. For .95<=|u|<=1.05, the old cube roots have magnitudes between .98 and 1.02. Their pairwise separation exceeds 1.66, since the exact separation is sqrt(3)|u|^(1/3). Radius-.25 disks therefore give the uniform old-root boundary bound

    |(z-r)(z^3-u)| >= .25(1.66-.25)^2(2.5-1.02-.25)
                         = .61134075 > .2.

The added-root boundary bound is

    |(z-r)(z^3-u)| >= .25[(2.5-.25)^3-1.05]
                         = 2.58515625 > .2.

Thus the same Rouché construction preserves A and, under the common embedding, every finite old word. This is a fresh scoped derivation, not a claim that the proposed numerical acquisition has run.

For the family Q=(z-r)(z^m-u)-epsilon, the compiler can derive

    Q_z=(m+1)z^m-mr z^(m-1)-u,
    m z^(m-1)(z-r)^2+epsilon=0,
    u_critical=(m+1)z^m-mr z^(m-1).

For m=3 this is a quartic critical-point equation. The compiler solves it, checks that critical values are distinct at its chosen parameters, constructs the same bounded generic isolating-loop/handle family for ALL values, and actually measures the resulting endpoint permutations. No special upper-new-critical-value path is seeded. Any failed handle, loop, conditioning check or root tracking is charged and can make the finite algorithm fail. Analytical existence does not rescue a failed implementation.

Store acquired generators and inverse paths in a graph of measured reachable permutations. Dijkstra search using measured execution work can enumerate distinct library words without re-evaluating equivalent permutations as if they were new capabilities. Task queries still execute the proposed word on the actual substrate; model predictions do not count as observed task performance. A path-preparation tree using known paths/inverses can supply reachable starting states without free reseeding. If instead the test permits independently prepared input branches, this access and every preparation must be explicitly given to all rivals and counted.

A discriminating training task is a hidden four-cycle T on the common transported anchor labels, such as (0 1 2 3), with the new state labeled 3. The evaluator returns only the fraction of four actual input episodes whose terminal branch agrees with T. Its label table is sealed from the learner/compiler. A single elementary critical loop is a transposition and cannot solve T; the old three-cycle alone also cannot solve it. The library must acquire a new interacting action and synthesize a word. Intermediate rewards need not all be positive: identity scores zero against a four-cycle, while the correct composed word scores one; incorrect words can score zero or partial agreement. T is a task specification, not a supplied path or a desired label for any particular elementary loop.

After acquisition, freeze the operational library. Evaluate held-out target permutations/words involving both old and new states, selected before training. The exact test need not exhaust all 24 permutations, but each measured claim must refer to actual carried-state execution. A complete generic library with a discovered old/new transposition and A can in principle realize all S4 by root's group argument. The task graph planner may use that fact to propose words, but cannot assume an unmeasured loop has the needed transposition.

The no-growth and no-coupling controls cannot implement T from the old component under the same continuation actuator. The no-coupling control may contain the fourth numeric root, but cannot move an old branch into it. An oracle-designed-path control measures the exact information advantage that the previous check supplied; compare its missing search/compilation work explicitly. The fully charged conventional polynomial/continuation planner is the primary equality rival. Under digital-output semantics an explicit permutation table is a legitimate cheaper implementation. A weak SGD baseline would not answer either comparison.

This degree-four proposal is the strongest exact next test in v3. It tests autonomous operation acquisition and task-selected composition beyond the manually supplied B path. It does not establish that the designed coefficient mutation was invented by a neural learner. The all-loop compiler is a strong classical algorithmic prior, and the complete pipeline remains classical constructive search unless a further learned organizational consequence is demonstrated.

## Exact next distinguishing test and decision

The next test worth doing is the degree-four operational-library arm above, because it addresses a gap not covered by the completed growth check. Freeze one structural proposal, the generic all-critical-values path compiler, reward interface and cost penalty; withhold all successful paths/permutation labels from the learner. The controller must produce and score its own critical values, handles and actual endpoint lifts, then synthesize its task word from learned effects. Reversals must restore the carried state after failed proposals. Let root set the externally enforced finite resource envelope and numerical thresholds before execution. The earlier six-candidate scaffold is not the proposed central execution.

Success requires (i) task-feedback acceptance of a nonzero-coupling extension and its own compiled interacting library, (ii) analytically certified and executed old A retention, (iii) no-coupling failure under carried-state semantics, (iv) a task word requiring composition plus actual held-out mixed words, and (v) complete acquisition accounting. The held-out words and all starting states should be selected before training. A compiler that cannot find an interacting generator inside its frozen finite path grammar has failed this algorithm even though an oracle-designed path exists.

Compare the symbolic growth/compiler on identical information. It is expected to match if the algorithms are literally identical. Such a result supports a bounded autonomous construction mechanism and clarifies its exact dependence on the designed structural prior; it does not establish neural superiority, novelty or a paradigm-level principle. Compare the FSM under digital-output semantics to expose how much the actuator restriction is carrying the result. A separate relation-fitting arm can later test continuous evidence-to-operation transfer, but should not be bundled into a larger test merely to increase the artifact count.

The important scientific residual after a successful construction test would be specific: can learned shared continuous structure make new operations materially easier to acquire or preserve than the full symbolic/model-continuation alternative on a consequential task family? No such consequence is presently derived. The present mechanism deserves this one causal test only if root judges the executable-coupling gap worth resolving; a pass should not automatically authorize another toy extension. This is a branch judgment, not a claim that the main campaign is finished.
