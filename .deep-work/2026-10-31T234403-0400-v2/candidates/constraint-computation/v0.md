## Judgment and connection to the question

Ordinary acausal neural equations plus adaptive elimination have a direct equation-compiler rival. A more consequential version makes the unresolved *branch* of a learned relation the computational state. Moving the observed/control variables around a closed loop can return them to exactly their original values while permuting the internal branch. Composition of control loops then composes operations. The internal computation is obtained from the geometry of an acquired relation, rather than from an endpoint predictor or a preselected forward/inverse direction.

This is a proposed neural acquisition mechanism, not a claim that monodromy, path lifting, reversible computation, or relation learning is new. The current construction changes execution organization, but effective acquisition and an advantage over a same-information continuation solver remain open.

## State and actual transition

Use real coordinates throughout, writing complex notation only for convenience. Let internal state u in R^2 and boundary x in R^2 satisfy F_theta(u,x)=g_theta(u)-x=0. g_theta is a smooth neural map. Its full relation, not an inverse function, is retained. Different u can be feasible at the same x.

The executable state is (theta,u,x), including the actual retained u. It is not a distribution over independently sampled roots. Along a differentiable boundary path x(t), execute

    du/dt = [Dg_theta(u)]^{-1} (dx/dt - kappa F_theta(u,x)).

This uses an ordinary 2-by-2 solve. Wherever the Jacobian is nonsingular, the chain rule gives dF/dt=-kappa F. Thus an initially exact relation remains exact in the ideal continuous dynamics; off-relation error decays locally. The state is transported continuously from the previous branch. If the smallest singular value falls below a declared threshold, reject/slow the boundary step; do not silently jump to a new root. A finite numerical implementation would require a bounded predictor/corrector with a step-size condition and termination bound; none is executed here.

The branch state supplies the memory. Resetting u from x after each segment destroys the mechanism. Its history dependence is a central requirement, not a nuisance to hide in a readout.

## Acquisition law

Given observations (u_i,x_i), fit g_theta by ordinary gradient descent on sum_i ||g_theta(u_i)-x_i||^2 plus a smoothness penalty. Because x appears with a fixed negative identity, F=0 cannot collapse to the zero function. The source of u_i must be explicit: for the present proposal it is an observed internal coordinate during relation-acquisition episodes, not an unobserved variable conjured from outputs. Identifying useful internal coordinates from raw observations is not solved.

This loss alone does not guarantee the required topology. The mathematical consequence below is conditional on C^1 approximation over the whole lift corridor and on separation from singularities. Finite training observations establish neither. A C^1 acquisition criterion and its information cost are open.

A boundary controller may be trained through the transported dynamics. For example use a finite Fourier parameterization

    x_phi(t)=x0 + sum_k [a_k sin(2 pi k t)+b_k(cos(2 pi k t)-1)].

It closes at x0 automatically. For requested endpoint transformations over a declared finite set of initial branches, minimize endpoint error plus path length and a barrier against small Jacobian singular values. Differentiate through the dynamics, or use ordinary finite-budget policy optimization. The training feedback may prescribe an operation; the route in boundary space is learned. The following loops are hand-constructed witnesses, not observations of controller discovery.

## Full worked process

Take the exact acquired witness relation

    F(z,w)=z^3-3z-w=0,  z,w in C = R^2.

At the resting boundary w=0 there are three distinguishable internal states z1=-sqrt(3), z2=0, z3=sqrt(3). The critical internal points are z=+1,-1 and their boundary values are w=-2,+2. Remove these two critical boundary values. Away from them the three local inverse branches form a covering; no globally single-valued inverse is selected.

Define A: from w=0 travel along the real segment to w=1, take the unit circle centered at +2 once, starting at its near side w=1, and return along the same handle. Define B analogously using the handle 0 to -1 and the unit circle centered at -2 starting at -1. Neither loop intersects a critical boundary value.

As w approaches +2 on the real axis, the roots beginning at z1 and z2 approach z=-1. Write z=-1+v: w=2-3v^2+v^3. A circuit around w=2 changes the sign of the local square root; it exchanges these two branches. The third branch remains separate. Hence A=(12). Similarly, near z=+1 write z=1+v: w=-2+3v^2+v^3. B exchanges z2,z3, so B=(23).

Start in z1. Execute A then B: z1 -> z2 -> z3. Execute B then A: z1 -> z1 -> z2. The boundary starts and ends at w=0 in both cases, but the computed results differ. A static endpoint energy or predictor receiving only w=0 cannot specify both results. A stateful continuation rival can.

AA and BB restore every branch. ABA and BAB both exchange z1,z3. Concatenations of A and B generate all six permutations of the three resting states. The operations act directly on retained computational state; no lookup table or separate operation-result cache is required.

## Why this could matter beyond the witness

The mechanism offers a route from continuous learned relations to discrete reusable operations: operations are induced by loop classes and need not be individually fitted as output functions. If relation deformation preserves the covering and loops avoid the moving singular set, their branch permutations persist even though trajectories and coordinates change. That is a possible organization for retention and composition under metric changes.

The cubic itself is only a three-state machine, so reproducing it has little scientific value. The important prospective limitation is learning robust, composable internal transformations without training every transformation and composition separately. A credible next derivation must obtain interacting operations on a number of registers that grows with representation size, rather than merely a bigger finite label permutation. It must also account for the work of acquiring the relation and discovering safe loops. No exponential compression or learning efficiency is asserted.

## Strongest specified rival and decisive weak step

Give the rival exactly the same learned g_theta, Jacobians, retained u, boundary paths, arithmetic, precision and per-step budget. A classical predictor/corrector continuation method executes exactly the same branch transport. This is a mechanistic rival, not merely universal simulability. A generic stateful factor-graph inference engine with the same continuation rule also matches it. Accordingly the bare transition is not a new inference algorithm.

The neural residual stores a learned computational substrate. To count as a consequential new learning organization, it must acquire useful topology and safe composable operations at a cost that a strong ordinary learned dynamical system or continuation compiler cannot already match. Present v0 contains no derivation of that advantage.
