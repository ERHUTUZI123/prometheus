# R v2 — Construct the space in which a mechanism can learn

**AGENT PROPOSAL; HYPOTHESIS; reopened constructive branch.** The acquired resource should contain an execution algebra and an edit/credit carrier, closed under the same compositions. Useful task credit that escapes the current carrier requests a structural extension. This changes R from “learn rules that choose a data structure” to “learn an organization of computation that supplies its own affordable, coordinated variations.” Ordinary calculus, matrix operations and closure algorithms are designed ingredients; the proposed organization and its acquisition are the scientific question.

V0/v1 and their negative judgments remain archived. Their conclusion that a bare rule proposer reduces to neural-guided synthesis still applies to that bare proposer; it does not settle this new interaction.

## 1. Capability and mechanism are different claims

**Capability sought:** acquire, retain and compose internal operators, then adapt them on later tasks involving longer or unfamiliar program combinations.

**Proposed computational mechanism:** represent both a computation and its possible edits in acquired finite bases. Composition updates their coefficients using multiplication and left/right transport tables. Grow those bases when a useful edit or its transported effect cannot be represented. The retained object is thus an executor plus a reusable local learning mechanism.

**Proposed acquisition mechanism:** neural proposals and ordinary task gradients select primitive operators, shared edit directions and scoped relations. A future-adaptation objective penalizes actual execution, credit and compilation cost. It rewards useful subsequent adaptation, rather than small credit dimension by itself. Basis closure is an explicit algebraic operation, not an opaque semantic compiler.

No new computability, open-ended evolution or automatic improvement is claimed. A tiny carrier that cannot express future learning is failure, not efficiency.

## 2. Explicit inspectable realization

Let V be a finite real state space for one mechanism version. Neural primitives include learned linear operators T_a on V, possibly selected by a nonlinear neural controller. Fixed matrix arithmetic is allowed. For the exact derivation, the action sequence is held fixed during a program; policy-gradient effects of changing the discrete sequence are a separate channel.

Let A be the finite operator algebra generated by the current T_a and identity, represented by basis B_1,…,B_s. Actual operator composition gives a table

`B_i B_j = sum_k c_ij^k B_k`.

Let D_k be candidate *shared edits*: each k specifies an actual simultaneous perturbation D_(a,k) to one or more primitive T_a. Their parameter identities are explicit, not inferred from similar numerical activations. Let M be a vector space of edit operators containing the D_(a,k), with basis E_1,…,E_r, such that

`A M subset M` and `M A subset M`.

Compile transport tables from actual products:

`B_i E_r = sum_q L_ir^q E_q`,

`E_r B_i = sum_q R_ri^q E_q`.

This is a bimodule in standard mathematical language; the operative content is closure of edits under inserting a computation before or after them. The tables must satisfy the compatibility identities inherited from actual multiplication. Unverified learned coefficient tables cannot simply be assumed associative or correct.

State of a retained mechanism is

`S = (T, A_basis, M_basis, c, L, R, edit_ownership, scoped_contracts, versions, budgets)`.

For a computation a and one directional edit m, execute paired values by

`(a,m) * (b,n) = (ab, a n + m b)`.

The second component is not an invented gradient: it is the exact product rule. Its associativity follows by expanding both three-factor bracketings to

`(abc, ab p + a n c + m b c)`.

The expansion relies on the checked left/right actions. Starting each primitive as `(T_a,D_(a,k))` gives, for any finite word w,

`m_w^(k) = d/d epsilon T_w(T + epsilon D_k) at epsilon=0`.

A task output `y = o^T T_w x` yields directional credit `g_k = dL/dy * o^T m_w^(k) x`; add direct readout/input terms if they are learned. Update the owned edit coordinates by `xi_k <- xi_k - eta g_k`, then update their actual primitive parameters through the recorded D_(a,k). Multiple edit coordinates require multiple sensitivities or an explicitly accounted linear map; one small M does not magically supply all p parameter derivatives for free.

Weights stay fixed during a word when claiming this exact derivative. Changing weights after each token changes the differentiation problem; exact credit through the training dynamics would require augmenting the state. This limitation is not hidden by calling the carrier online.

## 3. Actual closure and structural update laws

For a finite matrix realization, closure is explicit:

`M_0 = span{D_(a,k)}`;

`M_(t+1) = span(M_t union {T_a E, E T_a : E in a basis of M_t, all a})`.

Stop on rank stability or the external budget. At most `(dim V)^2` independent matrix directions can appear. Rational elimination can certify small exact cases; floating rank thresholds are numerical hypotheses and require residual/conditioning records. Building A uses the analogous multiplication closure. This may cost more than the subsequent learning saves.

For a new task, compute available credit and periodically obtain a separately accounted dense gradient, directional sketch or replay probe. A missing useful direction is the residual of that information outside the current owned edit span. A concrete simple proposal is its largest residual singular component, or its largest coordinate in a fixed sparse matrix dictionary. Add the proposed direction, run closure, and compare actual later adaptation gain against all added costs. Dense probing is not free, and gradient-directed sparse growth itself is existing machinery.

The network may also propose scoped operational relations F_R(T)=0 worth protecting during later learning. In finite multilinear/operator families, these can be compiled into finite coefficient equations. A first-order lawful edit obeys

`J_F(T) deltaT = 0`.

The Euclidean projected task update is `deltaT=-eta P_(ker J_F) grad L`, with a bounded retraction/check if exact finite-step preservation is claimed. This is classical constrained optimization. The proposed object being protected is a computational relation, not merely a buffer of old output examples.

For a relation T_u=T_v at the current parameters, its lawful first-order variation preserves every contextual identity `T_p T_u T_q=T_p T_v T_q`: the product-rule terms involving changes of T_p or T_q multiply the existing zero difference, and the middle term is zero by J_F deltaT=0. One finite relation therefore coordinates variation over arbitrarily many finite composed contexts. This is a mathematical interface, not a guarantee that all task outputs are retained.

If the allowed descent is weak, that does not prove incapacity: curvature, conditioning or a poor parameterization may explain it. Treat it as a proposal signal for growth, not an impossibility certificate. A structural extension may retain old states under an injection i with `T'_a i = i T_a` for old actions, then introduce new action channels outside that old scope. Old laws are not assumed to govern every newly invented operator.

## 4. A concrete computation–learning interaction

Use matrix units E_ij (one at row i, column j), so `E_ij E_kl = delta_jk E_il`. These are coordinates for explanation, not a memory catalogue supplied to a learner.

Suppose A already contains diagonal mode operators and X=E12. A new task has input e3, output readout e1, and composition `X B`. Its loss is `0.5*(e1^T X B e3 - 1)^2`, initially with B=0. The dense task gradient at B=0 is -E23. Thus a generic largest-gradient coordinate proposal discovers Y=E23 from task feedback; it was not handed the target equation or a named data structure.

The initiating edit is Y, but its credit after preceding X is `X Y=E13`. A carrier that stores only Y and projects transported edits back to span{Y} reports zero derivative for the task. Closure adds E13, making the derivative exact. The new channel expresses the combined effect of two operations, not another independent parameter or a renamed state.

After a finite update makes Y a nonzero execution operator, forward closure likewise requires E13. The execution organization and its learning organization now change together. A generic dense matrix learner or full forward AD already gets the derivative right; those are mandatory positive comparators. The new prospect is acquiring and retaining the small closed carrier rather than keeping every dense derivative or rediscovering transport from each whole trace.

A useful related construction starts with two independent modes `(a1,a2)` and adds an interaction m with product

`(a1,a2,m)*(b1,b2,n)=(a1 b1,a2 b2,a1 n+m b2)`.

This is the upper-triangular 2x2 algebra. It preserves the old diagonal subalgebra, supports new order-sensitive interactions, and uses the same transport law for credit. It is not a pure change of coordinates: the old commutative algebra cannot represent these noncommuting operations. The construction is classical; the proposed learning loop must acquire when and where such an interaction is useful without a prelisted triangular-memory template.

The square-zero interaction in that three-coordinate example permits at most one such off-diagonal interaction per product. More interacting edits require new product channels (as E12 and E23 require E13), or the construction loses higher-order effects. This is a substantive limit, not a minor implementation detail.

## 5. Why this might matter beyond the example

Consider families of variable-length weighted operator programs with many reusable modes but only a small number of useful adaptable interactions. If their execution algebra and transported edit space remain small, the paired recursion carries exact selected credit for arbitrary word lengths without storing the word's trajectory. Its state size depends on the learned carrier and number of edit coordinates, rather than history length. Exact RTRL already avoids history storage; the possible advantage is smaller *structured* sensitivity storage and reuse across later programs, not being the first online derivative method.

The ambitious prospect is that networks can learn arrangements of internal computation for which future useful variations are cheap and coherent. A resource is selected partly because its own learning closes compactly. This directly serves the Fundamental Question; it is not only present-task adaptation or a data-structure demonstration.

An explicit outer objective is

`E_new tasks [ L_after_K_inner_updates + lambda C_execute + mu C_credit + nu C_proposal_and_compile ]`.

Neural parameters may propose T, edit ownership and contract scope; structural proposals add/remove bases or ports under a finite envelope. Inner updates use the actual paired credit. All task queries, failed closures, dense gradient probes, arithmetic, neural training, rank tests and retained versions enter cost. No “small basis” proxy substitutes for successful later learning.

The matrix class is broad enough to include weighted finite transducers and many modular recurrent operators, but not arbitrary nonlinear state transitions. For nonlinear neural components, closure must include their state-dependent Jacobians or use explicitly local/empirical approximations. Exact global claims do not transfer automatically.

## 6. Strong attacks and real rivals

- **Exact sparse forward AD/RTRL:** once given the same operator structure, a competent implementation may infer or exploit the same closure and match every gain. This is the strongest exact comparator. The acquisition and amortization advantage over it remains OPEN.
- **UORO/NoBackTrack:** these supply cheap streaming sensitivity estimates without requiring exact small closure. Compare actual cost and variance, not exactness alone. A carrier that expands to the full space may lose decisively.
- **Dynamic sparse training (RigL-style):** task-gradient-driven birth of a missing matrix direction is not new. R's proposed extra object is its closed edit transport and preservation scope, with derived channels that can be necessary even though they are not newly trainable weights.
- **Learnable monoids:** known approaches learn associative/commutative aggregators and exploit composition depth. R cannot claim that general principle. V2's issue is acquiring reusable spaces of legal/transported edits and growing them with useful new interactions.
- **Function-preserving growth (Net2Net):** old function preservation during growth is established. R must earn a difference through continued law-scoped plasticity and portable credit, not by duplicating units or merely increasing capacity.

Exact semantics-equivalent rewriting alone leaves exact derivatives with respect to fixed parameters unchanged. V2 changes the retained representation of edits and the available shared parameter variations; it must not attribute a learning effect to syntax alone. Pure conjugacy/gauge changes count as re-encoding unless they demonstrably change finite-budget learning cost.

Small exact closure can also be a trap. If A is the full matrix algebra, any nonzero subspace closed under all left and right multiplication is the full matrix space: taking E_pi M E_jq isolates any nonzero entry and yields every E_pq. Thus no universal exact compression is possible. Learning must discover/use a structured computational organization, accept approximation, or pay full credit cost. Protecting accidental relations can also block new functions; contract scopes must be revisable or conservatively extended.

## 7. Hardest missing step and status

The serious missing construction is acquisition of a useful compact execution/edit organization from feedback when the needed future interactions are not known. This includes finding shared edit ownership, avoiding accidental low-rank singularities that vanish after one update, maintaining closure through actual parameter changes, and selecting lawful extensions without a semantic oracle.

The current realization can make closure valid for an entire inner-update family by compiling A from both base operators and allowed edit generators, then closing M under that A. Otherwise a basis valid only at the current weights must be rechecked after every update. The first option may immediately become full-dimensional; the second may destroy amortization. This tradeoff is central.

**OPEN, constructive v2.** This is now an explicit interaction between computation and learning, with a worked functional effect, update laws, a broad conditional class and strong actual comparators. The algebra/AD pieces are classical. Neural acquisition, useful cost reduction and novelty are unverified. Continue from this mechanism rather than claiming a successful new principle or closing it solely because its ingredients are known.
