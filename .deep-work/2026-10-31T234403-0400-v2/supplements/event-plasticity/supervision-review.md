## Independent mathematical checks

These are analytic reconstructions from the stated rules, with no numerical experiment or theorem-prover execution.

1. **Reciprocal cancellation:** subtracting matched cross-map updates gives `E_v^+=(1-gamma)E_v`. Discrete contraction requires `0<gamma<2`; positive gamma alone suffices only for the continuous-time decay statement. Unequal rates or decays add forcing terms. The pseudoinverse bridge and projector identities in Appendix A correctly expose why this cancellation alone does not control pathway error, conditioning or the depth products.
2. **Ascent witness:** the supplement's two-scalar-forward-weight state has valid output feedback, full-rank cross-maps and `E_v=0`, but pathway mismatch `E_p=2`. With `gamma=0.1 eta`, its updated output is `0.1+0.079 eta-0.0099 eta^2`, strictly greater than `0.1` for `0<eta<1`; squared loss therefore rises. Gates stay in the stipulated positive regime. This is a valid one-step state witness to the missing implication “reciprocal agreement implies task descent.” It neither contradicts the conditional theorem nor establishes persistent failure.
3. **Gram update:** for `C^+=alpha C+eta a b^T`, expansion gives

   `H^+=alpha^2 H+alpha eta[(Cb)a^T+a(Cb)^T]+eta^2 ||b||^2 aa^T`.

   The equal-Gram pair `C=(1,0)`, `C'=(0,1)` with fixed `b=(1,0)^T` has distinct instructive currents and, generically, distinct next H. Thus old H alone does not determine this new coordinate-specific plasticity action. Supplying the actual current `Cb` and feedback energy closes this particular update; their availability must be accounted for.
4. **Eligibility information:** if `w^+=w+eta m e`, otherwise identical retained states with opposite erased e have different next weights for nonzero modulation. Retaining a trace/timestamp or collecting informative new observations changes the available information. It is not spontaneous recovery. This obstruction does not prohibit learning a different new mechanism from fresh evidence.

## A consequential precision refinement

The source note's phrase that “both reciprocal matching and pathway alignment must recover” should be read as a sufficient route to the stated generalized-SGD guarantee, **not as a necessary condition for useful learning**. Its earlier directional qualification is the right one.

For example, whole-operator `E_p` may remain large on directions never used by the actual output-error signal. Useful feedback only needs to produce beneficial owned parameter updates on the encountered task directions, subject to the particular claim. Fixed-feedback learning can also be useful without reciprocal matching. Therefore an eventual acquisition study should measure the actual teaching direction, task acquisition and retention; demanding exact full-operator alignment everywhere would overconstrain the question.

The supplement's gate-pattern inequality is algebraically valid under its stated fixed-weight, full-column-rank and linear-feedback assumptions: two different gates can impose incompatible whole-operator alignment targets. It is a scope warning, not a proof that calibration cannot work. Activity-conditioned feedback, restricted task directions or deliberately different learning dynamics are legitimate constructive alternatives whose costs must be stated.
