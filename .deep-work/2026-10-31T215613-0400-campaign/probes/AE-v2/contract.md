# AE-v2 acquisition contract — fixed before execution

QUESTION: Can a small neural policy, receiving only load, resource budget and its own assembly state, acquire a finite material-installation policy from delayed scalar functional feedback? The source recipe is inherited from E; neither chemistry invention nor boundary construction is tested.

GIVEN: E seed 1301; AE-v1's explicit source/sensor/carrier/load reactions and constants. A supplied private empty compartment, assembly machinery and two possible connector types are scaffolds. No m/z carriers, catalysts, sensor, output stock or connector are initially installed. All state resets between installations. The controller never receives A/B pulses, labels, internal task state, oracle installations or evaluator gradients.

STATE/ACTIONS: counts m,z in {0,1}, recipe r in {0,1,2}, sensor s in {0,1,2}, output y and turnover k in {0,...,4}, port in {none,direct,driver}; initially zero. Eight assembly actions add m, z, one recipe copy, .01 sensor, 25 Y, .05 turnover catalyst, direct connector, driver connector. STOP is ninth. A connector may be chosen once; other counts have declared maxima. Generic precursor consumption per action is 1,1,4,.01,25,.05,.1,.1 concentration units, respectively; every component is charged even if unused. Inert assembly offcuts, sequence-specific resource stoichiometry and physical synthesis work are not modeled. These normalized mass costs are a proxy, not joules. At most 15 assembly actions, then execute.

REACTIONS: m/z conformations and E's twelve conditional scaffold channels, with scaffold concentration r*C. Missing m/z carriers suppress their transitions. A direct connector exposes z to reversible load binding. A driver connector exposes z to the installed sensor, whose bound complex catalyzes finite Y0/Y1 conversion with kcat=1250*k and reset G=12.5*k. S=.01*s, Ytotal=25*y; sensor kon=100,koff=10000, load kon=10,koff=1, epsY=16,muY=32. All reverse and passive channels remain. Missing components can yield useless installations; feasibility masks do not enforce functional dependencies.

SCORE: five full reaction-model assays per installation, the existing AB/BA/A-only/B-only/empty schedule and weights .5/.125/.125/.125/.125. Balanced analog terminal score minus .0002*consumed precursor minus .0005*assembly actions minus 1e-5*weighted driven source+buffer work. Feedback arrives after assembly and all assays. This deliberately low-variance feedback contains more information per episode than one sampled Boolean outcome, but no action target or useful installation is supplied.

TRAIN: three fixed seeds 631/632/633, 2,000 installation episodes each, width-16 tanh policy and ordinary linear-softmax policy with identical observations/action space/episode budget, REINFORCE with prior-episode context EMA baseline and Adam .008; no hyperparameter search. Training contexts are load {0,1,10} x precursor {35,65,115}. Held-out contexts load {.5,4,7} x precursor {50,90}; no learning at evaluation. Freeze policy evaluation to 64 sampled installations per context. Both controllers receive the same resource masks. Independent random-generator streams and all seeds retained.

BASELINES: pretraining random policy; matched-budget linear policy; an ordinary nonneural random-enumeration allocator given exactly 2,000 reward queries per seed across the same nine training contexts, retaining best feasible candidate per context and selecting the nearest training context for new loads/budgets (with feasibility repair by choosing the best stored feasible candidate); exhaustive ordinary allocator over all 2,700 terminal states as an explicitly additional-cost upper reference. The exact table is evaluator-only: no policy parameter, action or baseline sees its argmax. Report all context results, material and work, not just best seeds.

VERIFIER: vectorized operator splitting of the actual six-state source/master-equation plus finite mass-action pools; exact subflows for source, reversible binding, and carrier conversion. Fixed step .02. Before trusting acquisition, independently compare eight stated configurations/cases with scipy BDF rtol 1e-9; maximum terminal-port discrepancy <=.005 and checked case-reward discrepancy bound <=.005. This is a sampled numerical check, not a uniform error certificate for all 202,500 trajectories. Also compare .02 with .01 on those configurations and check conservation/nonnegativity. One finite-difference policy-score gradient check on a fixed trajectory (no reward oracle needed). As an adversarial context check, evaluate every trained policy with the observed load replaced by zero while the physical load stays unchanged. No microscopic full chemical-state or physical fabrication claim.

CRITERION: report actual post-minus-pre reward and held-out performance for every seed. Partial acquisition counts only if all three neural seeds improve mean training-context reward by >=.05 and their mean held-out analog score >=.75. Failure is retained; no retuning or second learning attempt. No neural advantage is required or inferred. Strong contextual construction remains OPEN if learned policies converge to a fixed install or fail under variable load.

BUDGET/STOP: one inspected local numerical run under existing run_isolated.py, timeout <=120 seconds, CPU soft/hard 110/115 seconds, sanitized environment and denied network; fixed 2,700*15*5=202,500 reference assays plus 12,000 training installations (60,000 logical assays, generally cached), 6,000 ordinary-allocation queries, evaluations and eight BDF checks. These oracle/precomputation costs are extra and disclosed; caching changes CPU cost, not logical data cost. Writes only probes/AE-v2; no secrets, new services, large training, children or follow-on experiment. Stop with a certificate including all failures. This is a separate follow-on repair, not independent invention.
