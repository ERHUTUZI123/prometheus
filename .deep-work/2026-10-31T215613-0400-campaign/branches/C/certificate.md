**WEAKEST REMAINING LINK:** learning a behaviorally adequate acceptance signal from task experience and counterfactual traces, without putting the whole solver into a critic or hand-designing the operation semantics. A second weak link is whether deformable reconstruction beats frozen modules with equally powerful adapters and equal total budgets.

**FAILED BRANCHES:** mean-activity stabilization as a proxy for functional preservation is falsified in the explicit two-input counterexample. Short-horizon acceptance as a guarantee of long-memory preservation is falsified in the leaky-memory example. Neither is the claim that the richer learned mechanism must fail.

A future test must compare at least deformable learned-constraint operations, frozen operations plus adapters, and matched flat graph search, with critics absent from normal execution.
