# Closest-prior attack

SOURCE: Liao et al., LODO, https://arxiv.org/html/2210.06171 §3 Eqs1–2/Alg1 and §4.2. Already meta-learns neural positive symmetric preconditioning and uses positive definiteness in a saddle stability argument. Li et al., https://people.csail.mit.edu/yichenl/projects/preconditioner/paper.pdf PDF3–5 §§4.2–4.3, already learns graph-based Cholesky-type preconditioners under explicit validity and distribution-aware losses.

JUDGMENT: the coordinator mobility repair is a technically substantive change from B’s failed energy-augmentation rule, but its core learning principle is existing preconditioning. There is no defensible foundational novelty in replacing E by a fixed E with learned SPD mobility alone. The proof/observations remain useful to delimit what a purported self-improving computation may safely change. We did not reproduce those papers’ benchmarks or independently prove their full theory.
