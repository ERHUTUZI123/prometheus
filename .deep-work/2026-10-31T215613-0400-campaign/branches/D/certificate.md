# Branch D first-stage certificate

Judgment: **a coherent executable opportunity, but no substantiated new mechanism or full joint-discovery learner.** The useful direction is to make learned representations editable: construct recognizers and writeback semantics together so new internal objects become valid intervention targets. The v0 core reconstructs established lenses; the remaining joint type/interface discovery gap is OPEN and unbuilt.

## CLAIM | STATUS | EVIDENCE

**A learned representation can carry newly callable source operations.** RECONSTRUCTION / executable special case. A representation contains Get, Put and a declared edit domain. For a synthesized view program u, construct `s -> Put(s,u(Get(s)))`; nested views lower by explicit composition. Concrete `mrrjjj` parses to `[(m,1),(r,2),(j,3)]`; setting the last count to 4 decodes to `mrrjjjj`. No semantic `run` label is required by that code. This is computational construction over supplied general primitives, not a new computability class.

**Primary reading changed the proposal.** SOURCE-CHECKED. Copycat keeps permanent concept topology fixed and resets across runs (Fluid Concepts PDF 226 / printed 216), so its temporary perceptual flexibility cannot be relabeled persistent learning. GP II already evolves truth-table primitives (PDF 599–605 / printed 577–583), so learning call meanings is not novel by itself. SICP's actual failure continuations restore speculative state (PDF 365,367, §4.3.3), requiring proposed concepts to specify executable effects. Simon's representation examples emphasize both strategy transport and the real bottleneck, motivating total discovery-cost accounting. Full exact passage records and causal reconstructions are in sources/D/reading.md.

**The algebra and much of the loop have close precedents.** VERIFIED by primary-source comparison, not by reproduction. Get/Put laws and composition coincide with Foster et al.'s [lenses, §3–4](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~bcpierce/papers/newlenses-popl.pdf). [Optician](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.03248), PDF 2–3, synthesizes bijective lenses from formats/examples. [DreamCoder](https://people.csail.mit.edu/asolar/papers/EllisWNSMHCST21.pdf), PDF 4–7, learns libraries and a neural search guide. These findings close a novelty claim for the reconstructed core. They do not prove no useful remaining joint-discovery mechanism exists.

**A restricted synthesis found coherent boundary/field code.** EXECUTED OBSERVATION. Given an explicitly supplied two-field fold/decode scaffold, the local enumerator generated 318 Boolean formulas, reduced them to 90 abstract Boolean semantic classes, tried 630 boundary/writer pairs and 396 task field programs. It selected boundary `not(current == previous)`, writer count `n`, field updates `n+1` and `2*n`. Four winning formulas differ syntactically but coincide on the relevant homogeneous-segment behavior; a unique canonical representation was not discovered. Construction execution used 118,782 counted program operations plus 22,336 Boolean-node evaluations for formula generation; wall time was about 0.09 seconds on the recorded host. Counts are transparent interpreter conventions, not hardware/energy measurements or total research cost.

The successful run checked 2,194 raw task outputs in its post-selection domain with no mismatch. After removing exact training-input overlap and duplicate cases, **2,188 outputs on 1,094 distinct unused raw inputs** remain. It also checked 1,097 GetPut, 2,194 positive-count PutGet and 1,097 lossless special-case PutPut instances. These finite checks do not prove unrestricted laws. The probe had a supplied symbol/count schema, fold state, decoder loop and affine field-program family. It did not learn a full new representation type or train a neural proposer.

**Editable identity and efficiency are fragile.** EXECUTED COUNTEREXAMPLES / negative cost finding. A requested two-record view `[(a,1),(a,1)]` decodes to `aa` but reparses to one record, so that edit must be rejected or its identity semantics changed. A bag-of-symbol-counts loses order on `aba`. On the two tested tasks, explicit fused raw algorithms used fewer operations than the parse/edit/decode implementation; no synthesis-cost advantage was tested. A semantic interface alone does not establish useful future discovery.

## WEAKEST REMAINING LINK

Inferring the editable target schema and intervention rules from downstream tasks without supplying the answer through grammar/type/scaffold, while paying all generation, checking and runtime costs. General Get/Put search is combinatorial; finite law tests do not guarantee general behavior. The source/target format assumptions in existing lens synthesis are consequential. Whether removing those assumptions yields a significant, differentiated research problem requires further prior-work judgment.

## FAILED BRANCHES / ERRATA

The broad novelty framing is defeated by lenses, Optician, GP primitive evolution and DreamCoder. The claim that this probe discovers arbitrary new types is unsupported. Universal PutPut was too restrictive. Frozen v0's doubling string was wrong; corrected to `aaaabbcccccc` in errata.md and verified by an independent raw-character oracle. V0 was preserved unchanged. The first probe execution failed on an atom-token bug; its record is retained.

## NEXT SINGLE DECISION

Decide whether **joint discovery of editable types/interfaces** is scientifically significant enough to pursue against the closest lens-synthesis and program-library baselines. Do not authorize a bigger string benchmark merely because this probe passed. This branch's finite first stage is complete; the high-upside hypothesis remains OPEN.
