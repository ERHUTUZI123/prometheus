**DECISIVE ATTACK:** The honest frozen-old-functions-plus-state-adapter baseline emits exactly the same runtime transition programs and gate-count sums (4/4 and 6/6). Thus this realization does not establish C's distinctive joint-accommodation advantage. A fifth-action extension involving the otherwise irrelevant variable matches every old observation but differs on four new-action states: old causal laws cannot provide an oracle for new-task intent.

**CLOSEST PRECEDENT:** Actual primary pages for NOVA establish that codebook/state-field inference followed by next-state circuit construction is classical state assignment/sequential circuit synthesis. Actual IIT pages establish trained causal-structure alignment by counterfactual state intervention, with a supplied high-level model. CD changes the information source and finite schema search, not an established new learning principle. sources/CD/reading.md records exact inspected passages and limits.

**WEAKEST REMAINING LINK:** A useful incomplete neural causal interface must be rich enough to preserve relevant future behavior under edits, cheap enough not to reproduce the old solver, and sufficiently permissive for genuine circuit accommodation. Full finite state/codebook inference does not solve this. A trained neural proposer, learned continuous state editor and causal partial-state discovery were not implemented.

**FAILED/REVISED BRANCHES:** Lens round trips alone cannot replace C's behavioral criterion. Keeping a learned latent transition model as the evaluator can simply move the solver into the evaluator. Replacing that evaluator by executed source targets permits the finite construction, but replay then carries a complete old finite solver and the source-query/search debt remains. The diagnostic synthetic field repair uses these old targets; it is additional before-run implementation detail, not a separately preregistered outcome.

**NEXT SINGLE DECISION:** Whether the incomplete neural-interface discovery gap is scientifically important and differentiated enough to prioritize. STOP this CD stage; no further experiment is launched.
