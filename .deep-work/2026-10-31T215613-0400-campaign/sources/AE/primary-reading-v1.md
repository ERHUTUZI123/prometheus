## Del Vecchio, Ninfa & Sontag (2008)

SOURCE: *Modular cell biology: retroactivity and insulation*, Molecular Systems Biology 4:161. [Primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2267736/), especially the introduction's clock example and “Biological realizations of an insulation component,” phosphorylation/dephosphorylation discussion, equations 20–35 / Figure 7. Accessed 2026-11-01 UTC. Substantively read the HTML around lines 94–113 and 278–314; images containing some equations were not separately transcribed, so the exact mathematical claims below use the visible prose conditions.

SOURCE: the proposed insulation depends on rapid conversion, abundant carrier material relative to downstream sites, and sufficiently low input loading. Simply synthesizing another reporter does not preserve its timing under load.

CHANGE: AE cannot treat a membrane or a second output species as sufficient. The repair must include an active, finite-rate interface and its resource conditions. Calling that interface a new computational principle is defeated by this precedent.

## Mishra, Rivera, Lin, Del Vecchio & Weiss (2014)

SOURCE: *A load driver device for engineering modularity in biological networks*, Nature Biotechnology 32, 1268–1275. [Primary author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4262674/). Substantively read the opening mechanism, Figure 1 caption, Box 1, equation 1, and associated bandwidth discussion, HTML lines 118–160. The methods around lines 308–343 were also exposed/read but no independent reanalysis of the experiments was performed.

SOURCE: Box 1 models free output and reversible downstream binding explicitly; faster carrier generation/removal reduces the temporal distortion. The paper also reports an implemented yeast load driver, rather than only an architectural analogy.

CHANGE: the strongest simpler explanation for AE's finite repair is an ordinary load driver added to a compartmentalized reaction circuit. The experiment must retain the source/sensor binding reaction; an input supplied as an unperturbable numerical signal would give away the main problem. The adaptive installation of such a driver is not established by this source or by AE.
