# Primary passages independently checked by final attacker

Scope: selected technical passages only, no experiments from these papers reproduced. Coordinator notes were leads, not evidence. No independent-rebuild directory was opened. Paraphrases below deliberately distinguish the actual shared mechanism from differences still open.

1. **Liao et al., Learning to Optimize Quasi-Newton Methods (2023 version).** [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.06171), §3 Eq.1–2 / Algorithm1; §4.2. Checked: a neural factorization parameterizes a symmetric preconditioner; one-step objective hypergradients adapt it during optimization. The effectively fixed positive-definite case preserves a repelling negative-curvature direction. This directly precedes learned valid solver geometry, rather than merely being a universal simulation argument. BF's normalized late-force/Oja learner differs from LODO's update; its particular periodic-winding counterexample is not established by these passages. No LODO performance figures or full theorem assumptions independently reproduced.

2. **Memisevic & Hinton, Learning to Represent Spatial Transformations with Factored Higher-Order Boltzmann Machines (2010).** [Author PDF](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~rfm/pubs/factored.pdf), printed pp.1476–1481, Eq.2.1 and 3.1–3.8; pp.1482–1486 §§4.1–4.2. Checked: three-way energy interactions are factorized into multiplicatively matched learned filters; conditional inference and contrastive-divergence training are explicit. Training on shifts/convolutions produces Fourier/phase-shifted filters; inferred transformation codes act on previously unseen target objects. This is a close neural relational-coordinate and reusable-transformation precedent. It is not B's integer-row grammar, bidirectional torus constraint solver, or SPD repair, and those passages do not establish autonomous structural growth or an analogous safety guarantee. The broad coordinate-learning/relation-reuse goal is already substantial prior work.

3. **Frady et al., Resonator networks (2020).** [Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.03748), §§2–3 Eq.1–2 and surrounding mechanism. Checked: supplied VSA multiplication/unbinding and supplied codebooks are combined with recurrent codebook cleanup to factor composite representations. This bounds claims of novelty for recurrent algebraic completion/inverse use. It does not acquire B's candidate relation rows and is not a direct performance comparator for B's simple phase trees. No companion mathematical analysis or benchmark reproduction performed.

4. **Stock et al., Synaptic balancing (2022).** [Author PDF](https://ganguli-gang.stanford.edu/pdf/22.SynapticBalancing.pdf), pp.3–7 Eq.1–11 and task-preserving-transformation proposition; local-learning derivation beginning p.8. Checked: degree-one homogeneous RNNs admit input/recurrent/output weight rescaling preserving the full deterministic temporal input-output map while changing hidden-state noise sensitivity measured in fixed units. Connectivity is unchanged. This is close prior for BF's postfreeze fixed-physical-metric argument, not an assertion that all gauge changes are mere bookkeeping. BF's supplied central-shear/activity example differs; no novel operation or learning advantage follows from that difference alone.

5. **Del Vecchio, Ninfa & Sontag, Modular cell biology: retroactivity and insulation (2008).** [Original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2267736/), introduction's sending-protein/binding example and insulation analysis around Eq.11–19 / Fig.6. Checked: protecting a source and transmitting a load-resistant output are distinct; a fast, weakly input-loading intermediate can attenuate downstream binding effects. Low gain may protect the input yet fail the output. This is specifically the causal explanation of AE's G=1 versus G=50 effect, not a generic shared-components objection. No physical replication performed.

6. **Mishra et al., A load driver device for engineering modularity in biological networks (2014).** [Original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4262674/), Box1 frequency-response derivation and results around Fig.1–2. Checked: fast phosphotransfer and sufficient carrier concentrations provide a bandwidth regime of approximate load insulation; the authors implement buffered/unbuffered, loaded/unloaded yeast comparisons. AE's designed finite output driver is an abstract different implementation of this established role. Its source law was learned earlier; its buffer and installer were not learned in AE v1.

7. **Arredondo & Lakin, Operant conditioning of stochastic chemical reaction networks (2022).** [Original](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010676), Tuning probabilities / Operant conditioning / Future directions. Checked: conserved catalyst-weight populations bias stochastic decisions; environmental feedback reallocates those weights. A declared external environment supplies feedback timing/interpretation, and the authors discuss that computation boundary. E's trajectory-score training of a temporal conditional program is not identical to this decision-conditioning rule. The broad chemical-feedback-plus-catalyst-allocation principle already exists; the exact differences require a useful discriminating result, not relabeling.
