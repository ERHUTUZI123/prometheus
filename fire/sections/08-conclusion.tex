\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Prometheus investigates how executable operation formation and continual neural
learning can interact through monodromy. The developed mechanisms distinguish
class-changing acquisition, safe within-class deformation, task-binding closure,
and soft/native consistency. Bounded development diagnostics establish that
these components can operate together, including terminal-feedback acquisition
with a feasible calling update; they do not establish a general architecture
or independent topological advantage. Historical prototypes remain separate
from the fully fused Primary architecture, whose validation, freeze, and
five-layer retraining are still required. The central unresolved question is
whether this organization provides a consequential learning, retention, or
resource benefit under fair conventional controls. The manuscript remains in
progress, with conclusions constrained by executed evidence rather than the
intended outcome.
